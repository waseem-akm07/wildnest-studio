## 1. Executive Summary

This document serves as the official **Episode Brief (EP-001)** for the pilot episode of WildNest Studio’s flagship franchise, *Critter Haven Resort*. It defines the narrative goals, emotional arc, comedic beats, world-building scope, visual requirements, and production parameters before a single line of screenplay dialogue is written.

In Episode 001, Barnaby Q. Whiskers (the anxious Syrian Hamster General Manager) attempts to execute a flawless 5-star grand opening of *Critter Haven Resort* the moment his human owner leaves for work at 8:00 AM. However, a sudden water-bottle tube leak threatens to flood the Central Lobby Tower, forcing Barnaby into a frantic battle against rising water, cheek-stuffing panics, and his own perfectionism—all while racing against the relentless ticking clock of the owner's 5:00 PM return.

Episode 001 represents **Stage 4** of the studio's progressive production rollout. ## 2. Episode Goal

* **Audience Understanding:** Viewers instantly grasp the core premise of the universe: a secret 5-star luxury pet resort operates inside a plastic bedroom cage between 8:00 AM and 5:00 PM while humans are away.
* **Audience Feeling:** Viewers feel disarmed by Barnaby's cuteness, laugh out loud at his reaction holds and cheek-stuffing panics, and experience cozy satisfaction when chaos is restored to order before the human door opens.
* **Hook for EP-002:** Audiences fall in love with Barnaby's obsessive dedication and desperately want to see what eccentric pet guests will check into the resort in the next episode.

---

## 3. Core Theme

**Obsessive Perfectionism vs. Adaptive Teamwork under Pressure.**  
The episode explores how trying to handle every crisis alone out of pride leads to escalating embarrassment, whereas embracing improvisational teamwork restores harmony.

---

## 4. Emotional Journey

```
  [BEGINNING: High Order & Pride]  ──►  [MIDDLE: Cascading Panic & Chaos]  ──►  [ENDING: Cozy Reset & Triumph]
  • 08:00 AM Alarm Chime               • Water-Bottle Leak Floods Lobby         • Leak Plugged with Gum/Button
  • Barnaby Inspects Clean Tube        • Cheek Pouch Stuffing Meltdown          • 04:55 PM Lockout Clean Reset
  • Bowtie Straightening & Pride       • 1.5s Frozen Reaction Holds             • Human Thinks Pet Is Sleeping
```

* **Beginning (0:00 – 0:35):** Pride, anticipation, and cozy perfection. The human leaves; Barnaby emerges in his red twist-tie bowtie, inspects the pristine Lobby Tower, and proudly flips the "RESORT OPEN" sign.
* **Middle (0:35 – 1:55):** Escalating panic, vulnerability, and physical slapstick. A water bottle gasket bursts; water floods Tube Line 1. Barnaby tries to stop the leak using his paws, then his cheeks, resulting in a hilarious 1.5-second reaction freeze.
* **Ending (1:55 – 2:30):** Relieved teamwork, cozy satisfaction, and ironic reset. Pip the mechanic launches a rubber-band button plug; the leak stops. At 4:59 PM, the lobby is wiped clean. The human door opens; Barnaby curls up in his wood shavings pretending to sleep.

## 5. Comedy Goals

### Comedy Style Breakdown
* **Visual Benign Violation (BVT):** High-speed tube water sprays disarmed by soft cartoon physics and adorable hamster squeaks.
* **Pratfall Ego Collision:** Barnaby's regal 5-star hotel posture collapsing into wide-eyed frantic paddling.
* **Reaction Holds:** Mandatory 36-frame (1.5s) frozen reaction faces with pin-prick pupils before every major slapstick payoff.

### Major Laugh Beats Target (4 Core Beats)
1. **Beat 1 (The Pouch Expansion):** Barnaby trying to hold back a jet of water by stuffing 3 thimbles and a sponge into his cheeks, swelling his head to 3x its size.
2. **Beat 2 (The Bowtie Snap):** A water jet shooting Barnaby backward through a tube, causing his red twist-tie bowtie to spin like a helicopter propeller.
3. **Beat 3 (The 1.5s Freeze):** Barnaby looking directly at the camera with pin-prick pupils as a giant water bubble hovers over his head before popping.
4. **Beat 4 (The Instant Pretend-Sleep):** The 4:59 PM chime ringing; Barnaby diving into his sleeping hut and instantly producing loud snoring bubbles 0.1 seconds before the human foot enters the room.

## 7. Character Objectives (Barnaby CHR-001 Focus)

### What Audiences Must Learn About Barnaby
* Barnaby is the General Manager and Founder of *Critter Haven Resort*.
* He wears a signature red twist-tie bowtie and takes his hospitality job with ultimate formal seriousness.
* He suffers from obsessive perfectionism and panics when things go off-script.
* Beneath his pompous pride, he genuinely loves his resort and will risk getting soaked to protect it.

### What Remains Mysterious for Future Episodes
* Who built the original sky-tube extensions to the closet and attic?
* What exotic VIP critter guests are on the resort reservation list for future episodes?
* Where does Pip the Dwarf Mechanic get his high-tech RC car shuttles?

## 11. Runtime Recommendation

* **Target Long-Form Runtime:** **2 Minutes 30 Seconds (150 seconds)**.
  * *Reasoning:* Ideal duration for high audience retention (>70%) on YouTube, allowing full 6-beat storytelling without fluff.
* **Cold Open Length:** **15 Seconds** (The Human Door Closes ──> Barnaby pops out ──> Bowtie adjust ──> Title Card).
* **Shorts Extraction Opportunities (3 Dedicated Cut-Points):**
  1. *Shorts Cut 1 (15s):* Barnaby's cheek-stuffing water leak panic.
  2. *Shorts Cut 2 (15s):* The 1.5-second reaction freeze & water bubble pop.
  3. *Shorts Cut 3 (15s):* 0.1-second pretend-sleep transformation before the human door opens.
