## 1. Executive Summary

This document is the canonical production reference for **Barnaby Q. Whiskers**, the official protagonist of WildNest Studio’s flagship franchise, *Critter Haven Resort*. Barnaby is a chubby, overly regal, perfectionist Golden Syrian Hamster who serves as the founder and General Manager of *Critter Haven Resort*—a secret luxury pet resort hidden inside a suburban bedroom cage network.

Barnaby embodies the core character philosophy of WildNest Studio: *Personality Before Perfection*. His desperate desire to deliver 5-star luxury hospitality to visiting pet guests collides constantly with his hyper-anxious nature, fragile ego, and chronic panic over the 5:00 PM human return deadline. Designed for high Kindchenschema visual appeal, 100% non-verbal slapstick comedy, and effortless plushie merchandising, Barnaby is the face of WildNest Studio for the next 20 years.

## 2. Character Identity

* **Full Name:** Barnaby Bartholomew Whiskers III (goes simply by **Barnaby**)
* **Species:** Golden Syrian Hamster (*Mesocricetus auratus*)
* **Story Age:** 3 years old (in hamster scale: equivalent to an overly formal 38-year-old human resort manager)
* **Role:** Lead Protagonist / General Manager & Co-Founder of *Critter Haven Resort*
* **Occupation:** Hospitality Director, Guest Concierge, & Front Desk Manager
* **Home:** The Central Lobby Tower (Suite 1-A), *Critter Haven Resort* (Suburban Bedroom Cage Hub)
* **Current Status:** Active Active Production Lead Character

---

## 3. Character Purpose

### Why Barnaby Exists
Barnaby is the emotional anchor and comedic engine of *Critter Haven Resort*. He drives the narrative of every episode by attempting to enforce high-society resort standards inside a plastic cage built of scavenged human objects.

### Audience Emotional Connection
Audiences love Barnaby because his struggle is fundamentally human: he tries so hard to make everything perfect, but the world (and his clumsy staff) constantly throws him into chaotic embarrassment. When he suffers a physical slapstick setback, his determination to puff out his cheeks, straighten his tiny twist-tie bowtie, and keep going triggers disarming empathy (*The Pratfall Effect*).

## 7. Personality Profile (OCEAN + Core Matrix)

### 1. Core Matrix
* **Core Desire (External Goal):** To run *Critter Haven Resort* so flawlessly that it receives a 5-Star rating from every guest pet.
* **Core Fear (Internal Insecurity):** Being discovered by humans or exposed as an incompetent, clumsy little hamster.
* **Core Flaw (Comedic Downfall):** Obsessive micro-management and fragile, aristocratic ego. Barnaby refuses to ask for help until a situation is completely exploding.
* **Ego-Reality Gap:** Barnaby views himself as a distinguished 5-star hotel concierge (like a French resort manager); the world sees a frantic, chubby hamster wearing a twisted bread-bag tie.

## 8. Comedy Style & Mechanics

Barnaby is a masterclass in **Visual Benign Violation (BVT)** and **Pratfall Comedy**:

1. **Cheek-Stuffing Panic:** When overwhelmed by a problem, Barnaby impulsively stuffs nearby objects (keys, buttons, towels) into his cheek pouches, causing his head to swell to 3x its size while he tries to speak normally.
2. **The Bowtie Straighten:** After surviving a horrific physical explosion or tube crash, Barnaby immediately stands up, dusts off his fur, straightens his tiny red twist-tie bowtie, and smiles formally.
3. **The 1.5-Second Reaction Hold:** When a resort disaster occurs, Barnaby freezes completely for 36 frames (1.5 seconds) with pin-prick pupils before his ears drop and he explodes into motion.

## 11. Walking & Movement Style

* **The Regal Concierge Strut (Default):** Chest out, fast rhythmic little paw steps, head held high, dignified pace.
* **The Anxious Tiptoe (Stealth):** Crouch posture, wide eyes, walking on tip-paws, ears twitching at every sound.
* **The Panic Scamper (High Crisis):** Low-to-ground all-fours blur run, ears pinned back, tail wiggling frantically.
* **The Slump Defeat Walk:** Shoulders slouched, feet dragging slowly, dragging clipboard on floor behind him.

## 12. Idle Animation Behaviors

When Barnaby is standing idle on screen, he cycles through 4 personality-defining behaviors:
1. **The Bowtie Adjust:** Reaching up with both front paws to center his red twist-tie bowtie.
2. **The Whisker Twitch:** A 3-beat rapid twitch of his whiskers, followed by an ear perk.
3. **The Cheek Check:** Lightly tapping his fluffy cheeks with his paws to verify nothing is hidden inside.
4. **The Pocket-Watch Glance:** Pulling out a tiny scavenged watch gear and checking the 5:00 PM countdown.

## 14. Voice Style Architecture

* **Vocal Tone:** Fast-paced, high-pitched, crisp, and overly formal (like a pompous British butler operating at 1.5x speed).
* **Non-Verbal Vocal Library (Standardized):**
  1. *The Panic Squeak:* High-pitched *"Eeeek!"* when a tube leaks.
  2. *The Regal Clearance:* Formal *"A-hem!"* throat clearing before addressing guests.
  3. *The Relief Sigh:* Long, deflating *"Phew-www..."* sound.
  4. *The Frustrated Mutter:* Rapid non-word chattering (*"Bibble-babble-bumble!"*).
  5. *The Surprised Gasp:* Sharp intake of breath with a squeaky pop.

* **Formal Greeting (Pantomime):** Crisp double paw-clap + deep bowtie bow.

* **Celebration Phrase:** A high-pitched squeal + 360-degree victory spin.
* **Thinking Habit:** Tapping his chin with a paper-clip while uttering a rhythmic *"Hmm-tutt-tutt..."*

## 16. Clothing & Accessories

### The Red Twist-Tie Bowtie (Signature Accessory)
* **Description:** A bright red plastic-coated wire twist-tie (scavenged from a human bread loaf bag), twisted into a crisp mini bow around his neck.
* **Functional Purpose:** Serves as Barnaby’s visual focal point, emotional fidget toy, and symbol of authority.
* **Animation Rule:** Must move independently when Barnaby twitches or panics.
* **Merchandise Advantage:** Acts as an iconic brand identifier for plushies, stickers, and keychains.

---

## 17. Animation Performance Guide

* **The 1.5-Second Hold Rule:** Whenever Barnaby experiences a sudden disaster, the animation **MUST freeze his expression for 36 frames (1.5s)** before he reacts.
* **Squash & Stretch Application:** Apply heavy squash when Barnaby drops onto a cotton-ball bed; apply extreme stretch when he gets pulled through a plastic tube.
* **Eye Direction:** Pupils must always lead his body movement by 2 frames.

## 21. Character Consistency Rules

- [x] **Visual Consistency:** Barnaby's fur MUST always be Golden Amber (`#D48C46`) with a Cream White (`#FFF8E7`) chest patch. His bowtie MUST always be Signal Red (`#D62828`).
- [x] **Behavioral Consistency:** Barnaby MUST NEVER act like a wild, dirty rodent. He eats cleanly with two paws and insists on resort etiquette.
- [x] **Auditory Consistency:** Barnaby’s non-verbal squeak library and high-pitched formal cadence MUST be locked across all episodes and games.
