## 1. Executive Summary

**Bao (CHR-006)** is a giant, squishy, ultra-cozy Panda cub who serves as Head Chef and Master Foodie of *Critter Haven Resort*. 

Bao represents the ultimate comfort viewing character for children and families. He moves slowly, loves naps, eats bamboo snack platters, and solves high-stress panics by offering everyone a warm snack or a giant bear hug. Kids adore Bao's rolling tumble-physics, his belly laughs, and his gentle heart.
