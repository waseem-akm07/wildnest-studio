## 1. Executive Summary

**Rocco (CHR-007)** is a sweet-hearted, clumsy, armadillo-armored Rhinoceros calf with a tiny rounded snout horn. Rocco is the muscle of the studio's character universe—he moves heavy furniture, lifts stuck water pipes, and protects small critters from falling props.

Despite his immense strength, Rocco is extraordinarily gentle, polite, and terrified of squishing tiny teacups or stepping on flowers. Kids love Rocco for his hilarious physical clumsiness (charging forward when excited, but trying desperately to brake) and his fierce loyalty to his friends.
