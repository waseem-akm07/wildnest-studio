## 1. Executive Summary

**Leo (CHR-003)** is a theatrical, dramatic, fluffy African Lion cub with a magnificent golden mane who fancies himself the undisputed king of every room he enters. Designed for supreme kids' appeal, Leo combines majestic regal pride with hilarious childlike dramatic vulnerability.

Whether checking into *Critter Haven Resort* as an Ultra-VIP guest requiring a golden velvet cushion or leading his own adventure series in the *Savannah Empires* universe line, Leo's giant ego constantly collides with silly physical mishaps, making him an instant fan favorite.
