# WD-0001

**Date:** 2026-08-02

**Status:** Approved

**Category:** Foundation

## Decision

WildNest will be built as an AI-first animation studio instead of a traditional YouTube channel.

## Context

The original idea was to build a faceless AI YouTube channel. After discussion, it became clear that building original intellectual property has significantly greater long-term value than simply publishing AI-generated videos.

## Alternatives Considered

- Generic AI videos
- AI facts channel
- Motivational content
- Random animation uploads

## Reasoning

Building a studio creates long-term assets instead of individual videos.

## Expected Impact

Every future decision should support building an enduring animation company.

---

# WD-0002

**Date:** 2026-08-02

**Status:** Approved

**Category:** Business

## Decision

The primary business objective is to build original intellectual property (IP).

## Context

Revenue should eventually come from multiple sources rather than YouTube advertisements alone.

## Alternatives Considered

- Ad revenue only
- Trend-based channels

## Reasoning

Original IP enables merchandising, licensing, games, books, and future expansion.

## Expected Impact

Characters and universes become long-term business assets.

---

# WD-0003

**Date:** 2026-08-02

**Status:** Approved

**Category:** Operations

## Decision

WildNest follows a documentation-first development process.

## Context

Creative ideas often disappear or evolve without documentation.

## Reasoning

Documentation improves consistency, onboarding, production quality, and long-term scalability.

## Expected Impact

Every important decision becomes traceable.

---

# WD-0004

**Date:** 2026-08-02

**Status:** Approved

**Category:** Technology

## Decision

The GitHub repository is the official source of truth for WildNest Studio.

## Context

Information should never be scattered across chats or local notes.

## Reasoning

Git provides version history, collaboration, transparency, and organization.

## Expected Impact

The repository becomes the operating system of the studio.

# WD-0005

**Date:** 2026-08-02

**Status:** Approved

**Category:** Foundation

## Decision

WildNest will operate as a professional animation studio rather than as an individual content creator.

## Context

The goal is to build repeatable systems instead of relying on ad hoc workflows.

## Reasoning

Professional systems increase quality, efficiency, and long-term growth.

## Expected Impact

The studio can scale without losing consistency.

---

# WD-0006

**Date:** 2026-08-02

**Status:** Approved

**Category:** Research

## Decision

No animation universe will be selected until comprehensive market research is completed.

## Context

Choosing a universe too early increases the risk of committing to a weaker concept.

## Reasoning

Evidence should guide creative direction.

## Expected Impact

The selected universe will be supported by research rather than assumptions.

---

# WD-0007

**Date:** 2026-08-02

**Status:** Approved

**Category:** Production

## Decision

Production begins only after the research, strategy, and creative foundation are complete.

## Context

Many creators rush into production before defining systems.

## Reasoning

A strong foundation improves production quality and long-term consistency.

## Expected Impact

The first production cycle starts with clear documentation, workflows, and creative direction.

---

# WD-0008

**Date:** 2026-08-02

**Status:** Approved

**Category:** Research

## Decision

Every research document must conclude with:

- WildNest Takeaways
- Things We Should Adopt
- Things We Should Avoid
- CEO Recommendations

## Context

Research should directly influence studio decisions.

## Reasoning

Actionable conclusions are more valuable than information alone.

## Expected Impact

Every research document contributes to improving WildNest Studio.
