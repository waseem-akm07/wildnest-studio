## 5-Phase Revenue Strategy

1. **Phase 1:** YouTube AdSense monetization (YouTube Partner Program).
2. **Phase 2:** Digital merchandise (stickers, avatar packs, mobile wallpapers) + brand sponsorships.
3. **Phase 3:** Physical plushie line (Barnaby Q. Whiskers) & modular plastic cage playsets.
4. **Phase 4:** Media licensing (consumer products, global broadcast, publishing).
5. **Phase 5:** Mobile casual gaming (*Hamster Haven Tycoon*) + theatrical/streaming partnerships.
