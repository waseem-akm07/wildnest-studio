# WildNest Studio

> **Building original animated universes powered by creativity, storytelling, and AI.**

---

# Vision

WildNest Studio is an independent AI-first animation studio focused on creating original, entertaining, and memorable animated series for a global audience.

We are not building a YouTube channel.

We are building a long-term entertainment brand where YouTube serves as the first distribution platform for our original intellectual properties (IP).

Our goal is to create characters and stories that audiences remember, share, and return to—not simply publish AI-generated videos.

---

# Mission

Create high-quality animated stories that entertain millions of people around the world while proving that a small AI-powered studio can produce world-class entertainment.

---

# What We Are Building

WildNest Studio will design and produce:

- Original animated series
- Cartoon universes
- Memorable recurring characters
- Short-form and long-form storytelling
- Character-driven comedy
- Family-friendly entertainment
- Original intellectual properties (IP)

Every project should be designed to grow beyond YouTube into additional formats such as merchandise, books, games, licensing, and future media opportunities.

---

# Long-Term Vision (5–10 Years)

Build WildNest Studio into a globally recognized independent animation brand.

Our long-term goal is to own multiple successful animated universes with millions of subscribers, a loyal fan community, and sustainable revenue generated through multiple channels beyond advertising.

Success means creating stories and characters that people genuinely love and remember.

---

# Our Philosophy

Technology is our accelerator.

Creativity is our competitive advantage.

Storytelling is our product.

Characters are our assets.

Consistency is our strategy.

Quality always comes before quantity.

---

# Core Principles

## Original First

Every story, character, and universe must be original.

We create inspiration—not imitation.

---

## Characters Before Videos

People subscribe to characters, not software.

Every major decision should strengthen our characters and their world.

---

## Story Before Animation

Beautiful animation cannot replace weak storytelling.

Every episode must begin with a compelling story.

---

## Long-Term Thinking

We optimize for building valuable intellectual property—not short-term viral trends.

Every episode should strengthen the universe.

---

## Build Systems

Anything repeated more than once should eventually become part of a documented workflow.

We build repeatable systems instead of relying on memory.

---

## Continuous Improvement

Every published episode teaches us something.

Every project should improve our storytelling, production quality, and creative process.

---

# Repository Structure

This repository contains the complete operating system of WildNest Studio.

- **docs/** — Studio documentation, world-building, production guides, business strategy, and research.
- **prompts/** — Prompt library for writing, image generation, video generation, voice, music, and automation.
- **assets/** — Characters, backgrounds, logos, fonts, references, music, and sound effects.
- **templates/** — Reusable templates for documentation and production.
- **episodes/** — Episode planning and production files.
- **experiments/** — Research, AI experiments, and workflow testing.
- **automation/** — Automation scripts and production pipelines.
- **tools/** — Custom utilities, helper scripts, and internal tooling.
