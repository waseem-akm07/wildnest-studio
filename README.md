## What Is WildNest Studio?

WildNest Studio is an **independent, AI-first animation studio** creating original intellectual property (IP) designed to entertain global audiences for decades.

We are **not** building a YouTube channel.  
We are building a **long-term entertainment brand** where YouTube is the first distribution platform for our original animated universes.

Our goal is to create characters and stories that audiences **remember, share, and return to** — not simply publish AI-generated videos.

## Our Flagship IP — Critter Haven Resort

**Critter Haven Resort** is a secret 5-star luxury pet resort hidden inside a suburban bedroom's plastic hamster cage network.

To the human eye, it's just cages and wood shavings. To the resident hamsters, guinea pigs, geckos, mice, lions, monkeys, owls, pandas, rhinos, and visiting critter guests, it's an exclusive luxury resort where animals live like royalty — but only between **8:00 AM** (when the human leaves) and **5:00 PM** (when the human returns).

### The Core Fantasy

> *"Step inside the secret, cozy magic of a 5-star luxury resort hidden inside a plastic hamster cage, where tiny pets live like royalty the moment their humans leave home."*

### Three Competitive Pillars

| Pillar | What It Means |
| :--- | :--- |
| **The Micro-Scale Secret World** | Universal charm of secret lives hidden right under human noses |
| **The Scavenged Craft Aesthetic** | Every prop is a re-purposed human object (sock = mattress, thimble = cup, twist-tie = bowtie) |
| **Pantomime Comedy Efficiency** | Language-independent visual slapstick that travels globally from Day 1 |

### Five Immutable Universe Rules

1. **Secrecy:** Humans must NEVER discover the resort exists.
2. **The 5:00 PM Ticking Clock:** All chaos must be repaired before the human returns at 5:00 PM.
3. **Scavenged Objects Only:** Every prop must be a creatively re-purposed human object.
4. **Benign Cartoon Physics:** Characters get squashed and stretched but suffer zero real trauma.
5. **Non-Verbal Communication:** Animals communicate through expressions, gestures, and squeaks — not human speech.

### The Story Engine

Every episode is generated through the **Guest & Deadline Engine:**

```
[New Eccentric Guest Checks In] + [Absurd Resort Demand / Failure] + [5:00 PM Ticking Clock] = EPISODE
```

This engine supports **500+ episodes**, multiple seasons, mobile games, plushie merchandise, and a theatrical feature film.

## Our Art Style

**Stylized Cozy 3D Animation** — Pixar's character appeal meets Studio Ghibli's environmental warmth.

```
[PIXAR 3D CHARACTER APPEAL]  +  [GHIBLI ENVIRONMENTAL WARMTH]  =  WILDNEST COZY 3D TACTILE IDENTITY
```

| Element | Standard |
| :--- | :--- |
| Geometry | Exaggerated rounded proportions (1:1.2 head-to-body ratio) |
| Textures | Soft velvet fur clumps, tinted semi-translucent plastic, cotton-ball plush |
| Lighting | 3-Point Warm: Key (5500K Golden) + Fill (6500K Cyan) + Rim (3000K Amber) |
| Eyes | Large, dark pupils with 2 crisp white catch-light reflections |
| Frame Rate | 24fps progressive; "ones" during fast action, "twos" during calm dialogue |
| Camera | Always at critter eye-level (2–4 inches off ground) — never from human height |

## Language & Audio Strategy

### Primary Language: Hinglish

Hinglish = natural conversational Hindi (75% grammar) + everyday English loanwords (25% vocabulary).

> *Example: "Boss, ye mission toh fail ho gaya!" or "Hamara plan perfect hai, don't worry!"*

| Rule | Standard |
| :--- | :--- |
| Grammar engine | Hindi-first sentence structure |
| English words | Only everyday words: *plan, boss, mission, idea, sorry, perfect, ok, problem* |
| Max sentence length | **8 words per spoken dialogue line** |
| Forbidden | Archaic Sanskrit, heavy Urdu, hyper-local street slang |
| Reading age | 6th-grade comprehension level |

### The 80/20 Visual-First Rule

| 80% Visual Storytelling | 20% Dialogue Enhancement |
| :--- | :--- |
| Expressions, slapstick, timing, body language | Punctuation, character flair, reaction squeaks |

> **The Silent Mute Test:** Every episode must be 100% understandable with 0 audio volume. If a plot point requires dialogue to understand, the scene is rejected.

### Subtitle Tracks (3 Required Per Video)

```
Track 1: Hinglish Roman Subtitles   ──► Indian Digital Viewers (Mobile)
Track 2: Hindi Devanagari Subtitles  ──► Traditional Hindi Broadcast & Co-Viewing Families
Track 3: English SDH Subtitles       ──► Global International & Hard-of-Hearing Viewers
```

### Global Localization Phases

```
PHASE 1 (Months 1–18)       PHASE 2 (Months 18–36)       PHASE 3 (Months 36–60)
India & South Asia           English Global Channel        Multilingual Expansion
Hinglish Primary             AI Dubbed English             ES, PT, ID, AR, JP
500M+ YouTube viewers        US / UK / Europe              LATAM, SEA, MENA
```

## Content Formats

| Format | Duration | Purpose | Beat Structure |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Micro Short** | 15 seconds | Viral hooks, single-gag visual loops | 1 Setup → 1 Punchline → Loop |
| **Short** | 30 seconds | Character intro, signature gag showcase | Hook + Escalation + Payoff |
| **Mini Episode** | 60 seconds | Full mini-story with emotional arc | Compressed 6-Beat Framework |
| **Standard Episode** | 2.5 minutes | Core episodic content, Season 1 format | Full 6-Beat Framework |
| **Extended Episode** | 8 minutes | Deep character development, A-plot + B-plot | Interwoven 6-Beat |
| **Seasonal Special** | 12 minutes | Holiday events, season finales | Extended 6-Beat with heightened stakes |

### The 6-Beat Hybrid Storytelling Framework

```
BEAT 1: THE HOOK  (0–5%)    ──► Establish normal life + sudden trigger
BEAT 2: THE BEND  (5–25%)   ──► First attempt + complication begins
BEAT 3: THE TWIST (25–50%)  ──► Major setback escalates out of control
BEAT 4: THE CHAOS (50–85%)  ──► Climax: extreme slapstick + 1.5s reaction holds
BEAT 5: THE RESET (85–95%)  ──► Teamwork resolution before deadline
BEAT 6: THE LOOP  (95–100%) ──► Final button joke + seamless loop
```

## The Production Pipeline — 14 Stages

Every WildNest video follows a strict 14-stage sequential chain from idea to retrospective:

```
[1. IDEA] ──► [2. BRIEF] ──► [3. SCRIPT] ──► [4. SHOT LIST] ──► [5. ASSET GEN] ──► [6. VIDEO GEN] ──► [7. VOICE GEN]
                                                                                                           │
[14. RETRO] ◄── [13. ANALYTICS] ◄── [12. UPLOAD] ◄── [11. THUMBNAIL] ◄── [10. QA AUDIT] ◄── [9. EDITING] ◄── [8. MUSIC & SFX]
```

| Stage | What Happens | Key Output |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Idea** | Concept aligned to resort comedy + character flaws | Approved 1-line logline |
| **2. Brief** | Complete `00_Brief.md` with goals, theme, flaw engine | Production brief document |
| **3. Script** | Hinglish screenplay enforcing 8-word rule + Mute Test | Locked script |
| **4. Shot List** | Scene breakdown with lens angles + AI prompt manifest | Timecoded shotboard |
| **5. Asset Generation** | AI image rendering (Flux/SDXL via ControlNet) | 4K character & environment renders |
| **6. Video Generation** | 24fps AI video clips (Veo/Runway) | MP4 clips with zero warping |
| **7. Voice Generation** | AI voice synthesis + non-verbal squeaks (ElevenLabs) | WAV audio stems |
| **8. Music & SFX** | Score composition + foley sounds | Stereo music + SFX stems |
| **9. Editing** | NLE timeline assembly with 1.5s reaction holds verified | Master timeline |
| **10. QA Audit** | 8-Gate Quality Inspection Checklist | All gates passed |
| **11. Thumbnail** | 3-Element CTR Rule (60% face + 30% bg + 10% prop) | 1920×1080 PNG |
| **12. Upload** | YouTube publish with metadata + 3 SRT subtitle tracks | Published video |
| **13. Analytics** | 24h/48h retention, CTR, audience data | Performance report |
| **14. Retrospective** | Document learnings, refine prompts, update CHANGELOG | Improved pipeline |

## Quality Gates — 8-Gate Inspection Checklist

No video is published until ALL 8 gates pass:

- [ ] **Gate 1 — Story:** 6-Beat hybrid structure verified; script passes Silent Mute Test.
- [ ] **Gate 2 — Character:** Barnaby matches locked hex codes (`#D48C46` fur, `#D62828` bowtie) in 100% of shots.
- [ ] **Gate 3 — Visual Consistency:** Zero set drift or perspective warping across camera angles.
- [ ] **Gate 4 — Animation Quality:** 24fps smooth render with zero AI melting/warping artifacts.
- [ ] **Gate 5 — Voice & Audio:** Hinglish dialogue cadence clear; non-verbal squeaks integrated.
- [ ] **Gate 6 — Music & Mix:** Stereo music balanced; audio mixed to -14 LUFS integrated.
- [ ] **Gate 7 — Branding:** WildNest 3D end card & subscriber callout attached.
- [ ] **Gate 8 — Thumbnail:** Passes 3-Element CTR Rule (60% emotion face + 30% background + 10% prop).

## Operating Principles — The Rules We Live By

### 1. Build an IP, Not Just a Channel
YouTube is distribution. Our product is *Critter Haven Resort* — a universe with characters and emotional connections that exist beyond any platform.

### 2. Publish Before Perfect
Market feedback beats internal speculation. A published video generating audience data is infinitely more valuable than an unfinished masterpiece on a hard drive.

### 3. Every Character Is a Long-Term Asset
Characters are not disposable visual effects. Every character we create is a business asset capable of generating revenue through merchandise, licensing, games, and media for decades.

### 4. Every Prompt Is Production Code
Prompts are versioned, tested, archived, and reused. A well-crafted prompt template is a permanent production asset.

### 5. Every Short Teaches Us Something
Every published video is a learning experiment. The retrospective after every video is as important as the video itself.

### 6. Optimize for Repeatability
If something works once, document it. If it works twice, systematize it. If it works three times, automate it.

### 7. Audience Trust Is the Most Valuable Metric
Trust drives retention, sharing, and loyalty. Protect it above all else.

### 8. Systems Over Hacks
Build reusable workflows, not one-off tricks. A solved problem should stay solved.

### 9. Technology Is Our Accelerator — Creativity Is Our Moat
AI makes us fast. Storytelling makes us irreplaceable. Any competitor can access the same AI tools. No competitor can replicate our characters, our universe, or the emotional relationship our audience has with the Critter Haven cast.

### 10. Visual Storytelling Is Our Global Passport
Content that works on mute works everywhere. Visual-first comedy makes our content universally accessible from Day 1.

| ❌ We Never Do This | ✅ Instead, We Do This |
| :--- | :--- |
| Chase trending memes or viral sounds | Build evergreen content watchable 5 years from now |
| Copy existing creators or studios | Create 100% original IP inspired by proven principles |
