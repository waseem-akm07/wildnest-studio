# Characters — Locked Character Assets

> **Purpose:** Approved character turnarounds, LoRA training datasets, and expression sheets.

## What Belongs Here

- CEO-approved turnaround PNGs (Front, Side, Back, 3/4)
- Locked LoRA model files and training datasets
- Expression sheets (9-emotion grids)
- Silhouette test renders
- Color swatch validation sheets

## What Does NOT Belong Here

- Raw AI generations → `production/current/CHR-XXX/outputs/`
- Review candidates → `production/current/CHR-XXX/review/`
- Character design documentation → `docs/04_Characters/`
