# Brand Strategy & Localization Documentation

> **Purpose:** Official studio guidelines for brand identity, language specification, subtitle standards, and international localization.

## Master Document

- **`Language_and_Localization_Strategy.md` (BRAND-001):** The canonical strategy defining Hinglish as our primary spoken language, the 80/20 Visual-First Rule, the 3-track subtitle standard, and our 3-phase global expansion roadmap.

## Key Brand Rules

1. **Hinglish First:** Natural Hindi-first sentence structures (75%) with everyday English loanwords (25%).
2. **8-Word Spoken Limit:** Maximum 8 words per dialogue line.
3. **80/20 Visual Rule:** Visual slapstick carries 80% of narrative meaning. Content must pass the Silent Mute Test.
4. **3 Subtitle Tracks:** Hinglish Roman, Hindi Devanagari, and English SDH attached to every video.
