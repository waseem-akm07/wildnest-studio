## 1. Executive Summary

This document establishes the official **Language and Localization Strategy (BRAND-001)** for WildNest Studio. It defines how every episode script, voice performance, subtitle track, dubbing workflow, and international channel rollout is executed across WildNest IP franchises (including *Critter Haven Resort*).

The strategy balances rapid domestic audience growth in India with seamless long-term global franchise scalability. By adopting **Hinglish** (natural Hindi-first speech with everyday English words) as our primary production language and strictly enforcing a **Visual-First Storytelling Policy** (where visual slapstick carries 80% of narrative meaning), WildNest creates content that captivates Indian families instantly while remaining effortlessly dubbable into English, Spanish, Portuguese, Indonesian, and Arabic without altering animation frames.

## 3. Primary Language Specification: Hinglish

The official primary spoken production language for all WildNest Studio content is **Hinglish** (contemporary urban Indian speech).

```
         PRIMARY GRAMMAR ENGINE: HINDI (75%)       NATURAL ENGLISH VOCABULARY (25%)
       ┌────────────────────────────────────┐    ┌────────────────────────────────────┐
       │ Conversational Hindi syntax,       │  + │ Everyday loanwords: *plan, boss,   │
       │ sentence flow, and verb endings.   │    │ mission, idea, sorry, perfect, ok*│
       └────────────────────────────────────┘    └────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### Core Hinglish Rules
* **Hindi-First Grammar:** Sentence structures follow natural conversational Hindi syntax.
* **Natural English Loanwords:** Everyday English words used in modern Indian homes are integrated organically (*"Boss, ye mission toh fail ho gaya!"* or *"Hamara plan perfect hai, don't worry!"*).
* **No Forced Pure Hindi:** Avoid formal, archaic, or textbook Sanskritized Hindi (*e.g., avoid "Door-darshan" or "Prabandhak"*)—use modern everyday terms (*"TV" or "Manager"*).
* **No Heavy Urdu:** Avoid dense, poetic, or formal Urdu vocabulary (*e.g., avoid "Zahaanat" or "Khusoosi"*) to maintain universal regional clarity across North, West, East, and South India.
* **No Regional Slang:** Avoid hyper-local street slang (*e.g., avoid Mumbai/Delhi specific slang*) to prevent alienating broader Indian and international South Asian diaspora audiences.

## 4. Language Style Guide & Vocabulary Rules

### 4.1 Allowed vs Forbidden Vocabulary Matrix

| Category | Allowed Vocabulary (Hinglish) | Forbidden Vocabulary (To Avoid) | Reason for Exclusion |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Everyday Nouns** | *Plan, Boss, Idea, Problem, Time, Hotel, Resort, Secret, Team, Mission* | *Karyakram, Yojna, Dushwari, Aijlaas* | Archaic Sanskrit/Urdu; reduces conversational warmth. |
| **Everyday Verbs** | *Check, Fix, Stop, Help, Relax, Focus, Try, Cancel, Start* | *Sanchalit, Anumati, Nishedh* | Overly formal; alienates young viewers and kids. |
| **Everyday Adjectives** | *Perfect, Super, Tension, Full, Ready, Crazy, Quick, Sorry* | *Adbhut, Utkrisht, Bepanah* | Decreases natural spoken rhythm. |
| **Slang / Idioms** | *Hinglish conversational expressions* | *Hyper-local street slang (Bambaiya / Dilli slang)* | Alienates non-metro Indian regions and diaspora. |

### 4.2 Sentence Structure & Readability
* **Max Sentence Length:** 8 words per spoken dialogue line.
* **Reading Age Target:** 6th-grade comprehension level (simple, punchy, rhythmic).
* **Pacing:** Clear spoken cadence with generous pauses for physical comedy beats.

## 5. Visual-First Storytelling Policy

Dialogue must **enhance the story, never carry it**. WildNest enforces the **80/20 Visual Rule**:

```
      [80% VISUAL STORYTELLING]                     [20% DIALOGUE ENHANCEMENT]
  (Expressions, Slapstick, Timing)             (Punctuation, Character Flair, Reaction Squeaks)
```

1. **The Silent Mute Test:** Every episode script must be 100% understandable when watched with 0 volume. If a plot point requires dialogue explanation to be understood, the scene is rejected.
2. **Pantomime Priority:** Emotional states, goals, and conflicts are established through facial micro-expressions, body language, and prop interactions.
3. **Universal Physical Stakes:** Conflicts center on universal physical struggles (e.g., fixing a leaking pipe, catching a rolling button, hiding before the door opens).

## 6. Voice Acting & Dubbing Principles

* **Natural Spoken Delivery:** Voice actors must perform with natural, conversational warmth rather than exaggerated "kid-vid" announcer voices.
* **Family-Friendly Cleanliness:** 100% clean, respectful, family-friendly language. Zero profanity or crude humor.
* **Distinct Vocal Profiles:** Pair contrasting vocal pitches (e.g., Barnaby's crisp, fast, formal high-pitch vs. Pip's squeaky, hyperactive raspy-tenor).
* **Non-Verbal Sound Sets:** Voice actors record an extensive library of non-verbal vocalizations (gasp, squeak, sigh, grunt, giggle) that remain identical across all global dubs.

## 7. Subtitle Strategy & Standards

All WildNest video assets must ship with 3 locked subtitle tracks:

```
[TRACK 1: Hinglish Roman Subtitles]   ──►  Primary Indian Digital Viewers (Mobile Feeds)
[TRACK 2: Hindi Devanagari Subtitles] ──►  Traditional Hindi Broadcast & Co-Viewing Families
[TRACK 3: English SDH Subtitles]      ──►  Global International & Hard-of-Hearing Viewers
```

### Subtitle Formatting Rules
* **Max Characters Per Line:** 37 characters per line (max 2 lines per screen).
* **Minimum On-Screen Duration:** 1.5 seconds per subtitle line.
* **Text Styling:** Crisp white text with 80% black translucent box backing for mobile legibility.

## 9. Script Writing & Translation-Friendly Guidelines

To ensure animation frames never need re-rendering for international dubs:

1. **Avoid Language-Bound Puns:** Never base a joke on a wordplay pun that only works in one language. All humor must be visual or situational.
2. **Match Mouth Movements to Universal Vowel Rest Shapes:** Dialogue scripts must end lines on open vowel sounds (*"A", "O", "E"*) that match neutral mouth rest shapes across languages.
3. **Leave 20% Audio Buffer Space:** Keep dialogue tight and brief, leaving 20% silence buffer in audio tracks to accommodate longer sentence structures in languages like Spanish or German.

## 11. Global Expansion & AI Dubbing Workflow

```
[Hinglish Audio Master] ──► [AI Voice Translation & Script Adapt] ──► [ElevenLabs AI Voice Clone] ──► [Wav2Lip AI Lip-Sync Node] ──► [Localized Video Master]
```

1. **Source Audio Separation:** Export clean dialogue stem, non-verbal stem (squeaks/sighs), and SFX/Music M&E stem.
2. **AI Script Translation:** Translate script into target language using AI models trained on WildNest character tone profiles.
3. **AI Voice Synthesis:** Generate target language audio using locked ElevenLabs character voice clones.
4. **Automated Lip-Sync Realignment:** Apply Wav2Lip / HeyGen AI nodes to realign character mouth blendshapes to target audio without changing body animation.
