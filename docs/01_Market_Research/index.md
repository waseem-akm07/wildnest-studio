# Market Research

> **Purpose:** Build a deep understanding of the animation and YouTube ecosystem before making creative decisions.

---

# Objective

The purpose of this research is not to copy successful creators.

It is to understand why audiences watch, subscribe, share, and return.

Every insight collected in this section should help WildNest Studio make better long-term creative decisions.

---

# Research Principles

- Study patterns, not personalities.
- Learn from successes and failures.
- Never copy characters, stories, or art styles.
- Document evidence whenever possible.
- Focus on timeless lessons rather than temporary trends.

---

# Research Areas

## 01. Top Animation Channels

Study successful animation creators and studios.

Questions:

- What makes viewers subscribe?
- What is their upload frequency?
- What animation style do they use?
- What makes their thumbnails clickable?
- How do they structure episodes?
- How long are their videos?

---

## 02. Storytelling

Research:

- Endings

## 03. Characters

## 05. Visual Style

## 06. Audio

Research:

## 07. Audience Psychology

- Why viewers return

## 09. AI Production

Study the current state of AI-assisted production.

Topics:

---

# Deliverables

By the end of this phase we should have enough research to confidently answer:

- Who is our audience?
- What should our animation style be?
- What production pipeline should we use?
- What makes WildNest different?
- What should our first universe look like?
- What can we consistently produce for years?

---

# Research Rule

No creative decision becomes final until it is supported by research or deliberate strategic reasoning.

We build with evidence—not assumptions.
