## 12. Dialogue Notes & Hinglish Policy

<!-- INSTRUCTION: Guidelines only. Do NOT write dialogue screenplay text here. -->

* **Spoken Language:** Hinglish (Hindi-first with natural everyday English loanwords: *plan, boss, mission, idea, sorry, perfect, relax*).
* **Sentence Length Rule:** Max 8 words per spoken line.
* **Non-Verbal Squeaks Mandate:** Interspersed with archived non-verbal vocal sets (*gasp, squeak, sigh, grunt, giggle*).
* **Mute Test Standard:** 80% of story understanding carried by visual action; dialogue enhances 20%.

---

## 13. Animation & Performance Notes

* **Frame Rate & Motion:** 24fps progressive rendering; 12fps for acting; 24fps for fast slapstick impacts.
* **The 1.5-Second Reaction Hold:** Freeze keyframes completely for 36 frames (1.5s) on peak disaster beats before punchline release.
* **Squash & Stretch Limits:** Max 180% stretch on tube launches; max 50% squash on hard impacts.
* **Camera Framing:** 35mm/50mm macro lens feel strictly locked at **2-to-4 inch critter eye-level**.

---

## 14. Music Notes

* **Cold Open (Beat 1):** Bright, cheerful 110 BPM acoustic woodwind stinger (`5500K` sunbeam mood).
* **Escalation (Beat 3):** Fast 135 BPM rhythmic pizzicato strings + ticking clock percussion.
* **Climax (Beat 4):** Chaotic brass fanfare with abrupt silent freeze on 1.5s reaction hold beats.
* **Reset & Outro (Beat 5-6):** Soft, cozy lullaby music box stinger returning to peaceful baseline.

---

## 15. Sound Effects (SFX & Foley)
