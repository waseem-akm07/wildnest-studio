## 1. Executive Summary

This document is the canonical **Master Universe Bible** for **Critter Haven Resort**, the flagship original intellectual property (IP) franchise of WildNest Studio. It acts as the single source of truth for all creative, narrative, technical, visual, and commercial decisions. 

*Critter Haven Resort* is an animated universe set inside a sprawling, interconnected plastic cage and tube network hidden within a suburban bedroom. To the human eye, it is just pet cages and wood shavings; to the resident hamsters and visiting domestic pets (guinea pigs, ferrets, geckos, mice), it is an exclusive 5-star luxury resort. Managed by an overly anxious Syrian hamster manager and a hyperactive Dwarf hamster mechanic, the resort operates in secret between 8:00 AM and 5:00 PM while their human owner is away.

Built to support 500+ episodes, multiple seasons, mobile games, plushie/toy merchandising lines, and a theatrical feature film, this Bible governs all future production pipelines to ensure 100% world consistency.

---

## 2. Universe Vision

*Critter Haven Resort* exists to demonstrate that world-class animation IP can be created by pairing AI-native production efficiency with timeless, non-verbal visual storytelling. 

The competitive moat of this universe rests on three pillars:
1. **The Micro-Scale Secret World:** Exploiting the universal charm of secret lives hidden right under human noses.
2. **The Scavenged Craft Aesthetic:** Every resort amenity is built out of scavenged human objects (sock bedding, button plates, thimble cups, key weights).
3. **Pantomime Comedy Efficiency:** Language-independent visual slapstick that travels globally from Day 1 without regional translation barriers.

## 6. Core Rules

The following 5 rules are **immutable and permanent** across all seasons, episodes, books, and games:

1. **The Secrecy Rule:** Humans must NEVER discover that the resort exists or that animals operate it. All resort activity halts instantly when a human enters the room.
2. **The 5:00 PM Ticking Clock Rule:** Every workday begins at 8:00 AM (when the human leaves) and ends at 5:00 PM (when the human returns). All damage and resort chaos must be repaired before 5:00 PM.
3. **The Scavenged Object Rule:** No manufactured human item exists in its original intended use inside the resort. Everything must be re-purposed (e.g., a sock is a mattress; a thimble is a soup bowl; a clockwork gear is a merry-go-round).
4. **The Benign Physics Rule:** Cartoon physics apply—characters can get squashed, stretched, or spun at high speeds, but suffer zero real-world physical trauma. They pop right back to form.
5. **The Non-Verbal Communication Rule:** Animals do not speak human words aloud. They communicate through expressive facial micro-expressions, gestures, squeaks, chirps, and non-verbal vocalizations.

## 8. Locations & Environment Profiles

### 1. The Main Lobby Desk (Central Hub)
* **Purpose:** Primary check-in desk, barter counter, and staff argument zone.
* **Visual Identity:** Built from a stacked set of colorful wooden building blocks, an upside-down tea saucer desk, and a paper-clip bell.
* **Story Opportunities:** Guest check-in disputes, lost reservation panics, and incoming package deliveries.

### 2. The Running-Wheel Fitness Center
* **Purpose:** High-octane physical slapstick zone.
* **Visual Identity:** Three giant plastic wheels linked with rubber-band belts to drive miniature fruit-blender smoothie machines.
* **Story Opportunities:** High-speed wheel jams, runaway hamsters launching into tubes, and fitness competition rivalries.

### 3. The Toaster Sauna & Cotton Spa
* **Purpose:** Relaxation and cozy character interaction space.
* **Visual Identity:** An old chrome toaster modified with wet sponge steam vents, surrounded by fluffy cotton-ball lounge beds.
* **Story Opportunities:** Secret gossiping, extreme melting relaxation reactions, and steam valve meltdowns.

## 24. What Does NOT Exist in this Universe

- [ ] **NO Talking Humans:** Humans never speak articulate dialogue on screen; they are seen only as large shoes, hands, or distant shadows.
- [ ] **NO Real Animal Cruelty or Trauma:** Cartoon physics protect all characters completely.
- [ ] **NO Magic / Supernatural Wizards:** All resort technology is grounded in mechanical scavenged human object engineering.
- [ ] **NO Language-Bound Puns:** Humor must function 100% visually without verbal dialogue dependencies.
- [ ] **NO Photorealistic Render Aesthetics:** Art style is strictly stylized 3D animation with clean shape language and vibrant pastels.

---

## 25. Universe Design Principles

1. **Character Flaw First:** All resort crises must be triggered by a staff member's flaw, not random luck.
2. **Scavenged Aesthetics Only:** Every prop must be instantly recognizable as a re-purposed human object.
3. **The 5:00 PM Reset:** Every story must respect the ticking clock of the owner's return.

---

## 26. Continuity Rules

* **Rule 1 (The Room Layout Is Immutable):** The human bedroom layout (desk position, window, closet) must remain identical across all episodes.
* **Rule 2 (Tube Map Integrity):** New tube lines must connect logically to established lobby hubs.
* **Rule 3 (No Permanent Human Discovery):** Humans must never gain permanent knowledge of the resort.
