## 1. Executive Summary

This handbook is the official **Master Visual Style Guide (ART-001)** for WildNest Studio. It serves as the studio's visual constitution—defining the artistic philosophy, target 3D aesthetic, shape language, color language, lighting profiles, material rendering shaders, camera framing rules, YouTube thumbnail CTR strategies, and copy-pasteable AI prompt style guides for all image and video models across WildNest IP franchises (including *Critter Haven Resort*).

Every AI image generator, AI video model, illustrator, animator, art director, and production partner must comply 100% with the standards defined in this guide. The goal is to guarantee that every rendered frame immediately looks like it belongs to the WildNest universe, maintaining an iconic, cohesive visual identity for the next 10 to 20 years.

---

## 2. Art Philosophy

WildNest Studio’s visual direction is governed by six non-negotiable artistic principles:

1. **Simplicity Creates Memorability:** Highly simplified visual forms, clean silhouettes, and uncluttered compositions register 10x faster in the human brain than hyper-detailed noise.
2. **Appeal Over Realism:** We do not pursue photorealism. We pursue disarming emotional appeal, disarming cuteness (*Kindchenschema*), and vibrant artistic expression.
3. **Warmth Over Spectacle:** Every scene must project a cozy, inviting atmosphere. Lighting and color palettes prioritize emotional comfort viewing.
4. **Readability Before Detail:** A frame must be 100% legible in 0.1 seconds at 16x16 pixel thumbnail scale before any secondary background detail is added.
5. **Character-First Composition:** Characters are the visual focal point of every shot. Backgrounds support and illuminate character intent; they never compete for attention.
6. **Visual Universality:** Every frame must communicate narrative intent, emotion, and comedic timing with audio muted.

## 3. Visual Identity

### Master Visual Impression
The WildNest visual identity is **Cozy, Whimsical, Tactile, and Visually Vibrant**. It combines the disarming character cuteness of top 3D feature animation (Pixar, Illumination) with the painterly environmental warmth and atmospheric magic of Studio Ghibli.

```
       [PIXAR 3D CHARACTER APPEAL]  +  [GHIBLI ENVIRONMENTAL WARMTH]
                                   │
                                   ▼
             ┌──────────────────────────────────────────┐
             │ WILDNIST COZY 3D TACTILE VISUAL IDENTITY │
             └──────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

* **First Impression:** A warm, inviting micro-world that feels soft, touchable, and safe.
* **Visual Uniqueness:** Scavenged human object craft aesthetic—every prop looks re-purposed from real-world items (sock bedding, thimble cups, button plates).
* **Brand Recognition:** High-contrast pastel color pop, rounded chubby character geometry, and 3-point warm golden lighting.

---

## 4. Target Style

* **Stylization Paradigm:** **Stylized Cozy 3D Animation** (3D geometry with soft tactile textures and painterly lighting).
* **Geometry & Proportions:** Exaggerated, rounded proportions (1:1.2 head-to-body ratios for mascot characters); smooth curves with zero unnecessary visual noise.
* **Line Quality:** No hard black ink outlines on 3D characters; soft occlusion shadows define form. In 2D graphic assets, smooth 3px rounded vector lines.
* **Visual Simplicity Rules:** Maximize smooth surface areas; eliminate fine high-frequency noise (e.g., hyper-detailed individual fur strands are replaced with soft stylized fur clumps).

## 5. Shape Language

```
      CIRCLES & SPHERES                  SQUARES & CUBES                TRIANGLES & ANGLES
       ┌──────────────┐                  ┌──────────────┐                 ┌──────────────┐
       │ Warmth       │                  │ Stability    │                 │ Energy       │
       │ Cuteness     │                  │ Rigidity     │                 │ Dynamic      │
       │ Softness     │                  │ Stubbornness │                 │ Danger       │
       └──────────────┘                  └──────────────┘                 └──────────────┘
```

* **Circles & Spheres (70% of Universe Geometry):** Used for character heads, chubby torsos, plastic cage tubes, button props, and cloud bubbles. Communicates safety, warmth, and huggability.
* **Squares & Cubes (20% of Universe Geometry):** Used for building block desks, shoe-box hotel suites, and rigid furniture props. Communicates stability and structure.
* **Triangles & Angles (10% of Universe Geometry):** Used for ear tips, cheek flare bursts, warning signs, and fast slapstick motion paths. Communicates panic, dynamic motion, and shock.

## 7. Lighting Standards

WildNest uses a mandatory **3-Point Warm Cinematic Lighting System**:

```
                              [KEY LIGHT: 5500K Warm Golden (50% Power)]
                                                │
                                                ▼
     [FILL LIGHT: 6500K Soft Cyan (25%)] ──► [CHARACTER] ◄── [RIM LIGHT: 3000K Amber Edge (25%)]
```

1. **Key Light (50% Power):** Warm golden sunlight (`5500K`), placed 45 degrees off-axis to create soft dimensional modeling.
2. **Fill Light (25% Power):** Soft cyan/sky-blue fill (`6500K`) to lift dark shadows and maintain bright visual cheerfulness.
3. **Rim / Edge Light (25% Power):** High-intensity warm amber rim light (`3000K`) outlining character silhouettes to pop them off backgrounds.
4. **Consistency Rule:** Shadows must never be pure black; fill shadows with 20% ambient environmental tint (`#1A1A2E`).

## 8. Materials & Textures

To optimize AI rendering stability while maintaining tactile appeal:

* **Stylized Fur:** Rendered as smooth, soft clumps with a subtle velvet sheen shader; avoid individual strand particle noise.
* **Plastic Cage Tubes:** Tinted semi-translucent plastic (`75% opacity`) with smooth rounded specular highlights and soft internal light refraction.
* **Fabric & Linens:** Soft felt, plush cotton-ball, and coarse burlap textures with visible macro weave.
* **Scavenged Metals:** Brushed brass, tin, and aluminum with soft edge scuffs; avoid high-mirror reflection noise.
* **Wood & Paper:** Warm polished mahogany, cardboard grain, and torn paper edges.

---

## 9. Character Rendering Standards

* **Eye Geometry & Highlights:** Eyes must feature large, deep pupils with **two distinct crisp white catch-light reflections** (a primary 100% white dot at 10 o'clock and a secondary 50% dot at 4 o'clock) to create life and intelligence.

```
       ┌────────────────────────┐
       │   ● (Primary 100%)     │  <── Eye Catch-Lights
       │      Pupil Area        │      Give Life & Intelligence
       │     . (Secondary 50%)  │
       └────────────────────────┘
```

* **Expression Subversion:** Cheek geometry must expand during cheek-stuffing scenes using smooth mesh deformers.
* **Shadow Placement:** Soft contact ambient occlusion shadows under feet/paws to anchor characters firmly to ground planes.

## 10. Environment Design & Asset Reusability

* **The 80/20 Rule:** 80% of series footage takes place across **3 Master Environment Sets**:
  1. *The Main Plastic Tube Lobby Tower*
  2. *The Scavenged Appliance Spa & Gym*
  3. *The Bedroom Closet Luxury Suites*
* **Environmental Storytelling (Clutter Pass):** Backgrounds must include lived-in details—dust motes in sunbeams, stray seeds, scuffed plastic tube walls, and tiny paw prints.
* **Background Depth Blur:** Apply a subtle depth-of-field blur (`f/2.8 equivalent`) to all background elements beyond 3 feet from the camera to force focus onto the foreground characters.

## 11. Camera Language & Framing

```
  MACRO CLOSE-UP (35mm/50mm)           MEDIUM ACTION SHOT                 WIDE ESTABLISHING SHOT
  ┌──────────────────────────┐         ┌──────────────────────────┐       ┌──────────────────────────┐
  │ Emotional Reactions      │         │ Dialogue & Slapstick     │       │ Environment & Scale      │
  │ Fidgeting & Expressions  │         │ Character Interactions   │       │ Central Hub Architecture │
  └──────────────────────────┘         └──────────────────────────┘       └──────────────────────────┘
```

* **Focal Lens Range:** Simulate 35mm to 50mm macro lenses to emphasize the charming miniature scale of the critter world.
* **Camera Height:** Keep the camera at **Eye-Level with the Characters** (2 to 4 inches off the floor). Never shoot down at characters from a human height except when showing human threat perspectives.
* **Comedy Framing:** Use stationary wide-medium shots for physical slapstick routines to let the movement play out without jarring camera cuts.

### 11.1 Visual-First Storytelling

* WildNest follows a **Visual First** storytelling philosophy.
* Episodes should remain understandable without dialogue.
* Body language, facial expressions, staging, timing, camera composition, and physical comedy should communicate the majority of the story.
* Dialogue should reinforce visual storytelling rather than replace it.
* Every scene should be reviewed with the question:

> *"Would the audience still understand this scene if the audio were muted?"*

This principle applies to:
* Episodes
* Shorts
* Thumbnails
* Promotional clips
* GIFs
* Social media content

## 12. Animation Style & FPS Standards

* **Frame Rate:** Standard 24fps progressive rendering; step animation to "ones" (24fps) during fast slapstick impacts and "twos" (12fps) during calm dialogue/acting.
* **The 1.5-Second Reaction Hold:** When a comedic disaster occurs, the camera and character **MUST hold completely still for 36 frames (1.5s)** to let the punchline land.
* **Squash & Stretch Limits:** Max 180% stretch on fast movement; max 50% squash on hard impacts.

## 13. Facial Expression System

All character assets must maintain visual consistency across **9 Standardized Expression Profiles**:

```
1. JOY (Enlarged pupils, high curved eyebrows, wide open smile)
2. PANIC / FEAR (Shrunk pin-prick pupils, wide sclera, jaw drop, sweat drop)
3. ANGER (V-angled eyebrows, squinted eyelids, clenched jaw)
4. SURPRISE (O-shaped eyes & mouth, popped ears, mid-air freeze)
5. SUSPICION (One raised eyebrow, half-lidded eyes, asymmetric smirk)
6. EMBARRASSMENT (Blushing cheek pads, squashed neck, eyes looking away)
7. CONFUSION (Tilted head, wavy mouth, one eye wide / one squashed)
8. SADNESS (Drooped eyelids, downturned brows, quivering lower lip)
9. MISCHIEF (Narrowed eyes, wide toothy grin, rubbing paws)
```

## 15. Visual Effects (VFX)

* **Allowed VFX:** Soft floating dust motes in sunbeams, steam puffs from toaster saunas, water droplet splashes, sparkling starlight dust, speed motion lines during extreme scampers.
* **Forbidden VFX:** Hyper-realistic blood, realistic fire/smoke, photorealistic lens flares, dark gritty grime, complex fluid simulations that break AI render stability.

## 16. YouTube Thumbnail Style Guide (CTR System)

To maximize click-through rates (CTR) on YouTube and social feeds, thumbnails must follow the **3-Element Rule**:

```
               ┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
               │ [ELEMENT 1: 1-2 LARGE HIGH-EMOTION FACES (60%)] │
               │                                                  │
               │ [ELEMENT 2: HIGH-CONTRAST NEON TUBE BACKGROUND]  │
               │ [ELEMENT 3: 1 ICONIC EXPLODING PROP (30%)]       │
               └──────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

1. **Element 1 (60% Area):** Extremely large close-up of Barnaby or main character showing intense facial emotion (Panic, Shock, Joy).
2. **Element 2 (30% Area):** High-contrast, brightly lit background (bright cyan tube or orange studio glow).
3. **Element 3 (10% Area):** One high-stakes prop (e.g., an exploding clock gear or giant dripping water bottle).
4. **Text Rule:** Zero or maximum 2 bold words in rounded 3D yellow typography (`#FFD166`).

## 20. Consistency Rules

- [x] **Rule 1 (No Photorealism):** Never generate photorealistic humans or realistic rodents. Style is strictly Stylized Cozy 3D.
- [x] **Rule 2 (Color Palette Integrity):** Main characters and core environments must stick to locked Hex codes.
- [x] **Rule 3 (Camera Eye-Level):** Cameras must remain at 2-to-4 inch critter eye level.
- [x] **Rule 4 (Scavenged Prop Rule):** All props must be identifiable as re-purposed human objects.
- [x] **Rule 5 (Reaction Hold Rule):** Every comedic impact must hold on the reaction face for 1.5 seconds.

| :--- | :--- |

## 22. Production Audit Checklist

Before approving any visual asset, storyboard, or AI render, confirm it passes all 8 gates:

- [ ] **Gate 1 (Aesthetic Style):** Is it 100% Stylized Cozy 3D (Pixar/Ghibli warm hybrid)?
- [ ] **Gate 2 (Silhouette Test):** Does the subject pop clearly in solid black outline fill?
- [ ] **Gate 3 (Palette Discipline):** Does it follow the 60/30/10 master color system?
- [ ] **Gate 4 (Lighting Quality):** Is the 3-point warm golden key + cyan fill active?
- [ ] **Gate 5 (Scavenged Prop Rule):** Are all props clearly re-purposed human objects?
- [ ] **Gate 6 (Eye Catch-Lights):** Do character eyes feature dual crisp white reflections?
- [ ] **Gate 7 (Camera Angle):** Is the camera positioned at critter eye-level?
- [ ] **Gate 8 (AI Prompt Standard):** Does the prompt follow master studio parameters?
