# Art Direction & Visual Style Guide

> **Purpose:** Art direction, visual aesthetic specifications, color palettes, lighting standards, and rendering guidelines.

## Master Document

- **`01_Visual_Style_Guide.md` (ART-001):** Master 26-section Visual Style Guide establishing Stylized Cozy 3D Animation, 3-point warm cinematic lighting, fur grooming specs, camera presets, and the 3-Element Thumbnail CTR Rule.

## Aesthetic Identity

- **Visual Style:** Pixar-quality 3D character appeal + Studio Ghibli environmental warmth.
- **Lighting Pipeline:** Key (5500K Golden Warm) + Fill (6500K Sky Cyan) + Rim (3000K Amber Backlight).
- **Master Palette:** Golden Amber (`#D48C46`), Mint Green (`#06D6A0`), Cyan Blue (`#00B4D8`), Signal Red (`#D62828`).
- **Thumbnail CTR Rule:** 60% extreme emotional face + 30% vibrant background + 10% high-stakes prop.
