## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

- **30-Second Audience Retention Target:** `> 70%`
- **Thumbnail Click-Through Rate (CTR) Target:** `> 10.5%`
- **Compute Cost Efficiency:** `< $15 USD` per Short; `< $40 USD` per Episode
- **Recurring Viewers:** `> 30%` of total monthly channel views
