## 1. Executive Summary

**Milo (CHR-004)** is an ultra-agile, endlessly curious Squirrel Monkey with a tail that acts like a third arm. Milo lives for high-speed slapstick acrobatics, button-pressing, and swinging through plastic resort tubes or jungle canopy vines.

Designed to captivate kids with boundless energy, Milo is the ultimate physical comedian—he cannot resist pressing any shiny button or pulling any loose lever, making him the catalyst for resort chaos and jungle mysteries alike.
