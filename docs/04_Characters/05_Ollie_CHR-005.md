## 1. Executive Summary

**Ollie (CHR-005)** is a round, sleepy-eyed, intellectual Great Horned Owl cub who wears scavenged monocle lenses and manages the Resort's late-night shift (from 10:00 PM to 6:00 AM). 

Ollie is the intellectual brain of the studio's character universe—he delivers deadpan witty observations, invents absurd scavenged contraptions, and can spin his head 180 degrees to catch chaos happening behind his back. Kids love Ollie for his adorable giant round eyes, sleepy yawns, and sudden bursts of genius.
