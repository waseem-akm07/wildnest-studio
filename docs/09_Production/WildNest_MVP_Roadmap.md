## 1. Executive Summary & Core Success Metric

This roadmap establishes the official **MVP Execution Strategy (WPOS-MVP-001)** for WildNest Studio. Formulated by the Chief Operating Officer (COO), this plan prioritizes speed-to-market, asset reusability, and progressive technical validation over premature perfectionism.

### The Ultimate Success Metric
The MVP Roadmap is successfully completed when:
1. **The First High-Quality YouTube Short is Published** on public channels.
2. **The AI Production Pipeline is Validated** with predictable cost, velocity, and zero workflow bottlenecks.
3. **Character Consistency is Proven** across multiple AI tools (Flux, Midjourney, Veo, Runway, ElevenLabs).
4. **The Team Can Confidently Produce Shorts Repeatedly** at a benchmark rate of 2+ Shorts per week.

---

## 2. COO Operational Philosophy & Rules

```
  [PHASE 1: CHARACTER LOCK]  ──►  [PHASE 2: ENVIRONMENT LOCK]  ──►  [PHASE 3: 5 ANIMATION TESTS]
                                                                                │
  [PHASE 5: EPISODE 001 LAUNCH]  ◄──  [PHASE 4: 10 YOUTUBE SHORTS]  ◄───────────┘
```

### The Non-Negotiable COO Execution Rule
> **RULE OF STRICT SEQUENCING:** No team member, art director, or animator may proceed to the next phase until the current phase passes 100% of its binding Exit Criteria. We do not build full episodes on unvalidated character rigs or unstable environment sets.

## 3. Phase 1 — Character Validation

### Deliverables
* **Character turnaround sheet** (360-degree front, side, 3/4, back views for Barnaby CHR-001).
* **Expression sheet** (9 standardized expressions per `CHR-BIBLE-001`).
* **Pose sheet** (Action, idle, panic, and bowtie-adjust poses).
* **Master Prompt Package** (Positive & negative prompt tokens locked).
* **Cross-Tool Consistency Test Report** (Testing output consistency across Flux, Midjourney, and SDXL).

### Binding Exit Criteria
> **EXIT CRITERIA:** Barnaby (Golden Amber fur `#D48C46`, Cream White chest patch `#FFF8E7`, and Red Twist-Tie Bowtie `#D62828`) can be generated consistently across 10 consecutive prompt runs in 3 different AI image tools with zero facial/fur degradation.

## 4. Phase 2 — Environment Validation

### Deliverables
* **Master Environment Model** (Central Lobby Tower inside bedroom cage).
* **Day / Night / Twilight Lighting Variants** (8:00 AM bright sun, 4:30 PM golden hour, night glow).
* **5 Locked Camera Angle Presets** (Wide Tower, Lobby Desk Medium, Tube Line 1 Macro, High Angle, Close-Up Counter).
* **Scavenged Prop Master Library** (Thimble cups, button plates, wooden building blocks, tea saucer desk).
* **Environment Master Prompt Package**.

### Binding Exit Criteria
> **EXIT CRITERIA:** The Central Lobby Tower environment can be re-rendered across 15 different scene angles without visual drift, background warping, or camera height perspective shifts.

## 5. Phase 3 — Animation Validation (5 Technical Tests)

Produce five mandatory short technical animation tests to validate character motion, physical comedy, and AI video rendering stability:

```
  TEST 1: WALKING           ──► Regal Concierge Strut & Anxious Tiptoe (Gait & Leg Motion)
  TEST 2: TALKING           ──► Hinglish Dialogue Cadence & Non-Verbal Squeaks (Lip-Sync & Facial Rig)
  TEST 3: LAUGHING          ──► Extreme High-Energy Joy & Belly Laugh (Squash & Stretch)
  TEST 4: PROP INTERACTION  ──► Cheek Pouch Stuffing with Thimbles & Buttons (Prop Collision)
  TEST 5: EMOTIONAL REACTION──► The 1.5-Second Reaction Freeze with Pin-Prick Pupils (Comedy Timing)
```

### Metrics Measured
* **Visual Consistency:** Character LoRA preservation score (Target: >= 90%).
* **Motion Quality:** 24fps smoothness with zero AI melting/warping artifacts.
* **Production Time:** Time required to render & clean up a 5-second video clip (Target: < 45 minutes).
* **Compute Cost:** GPU API cost per test clip (Target: < $3.00 USD).
* **AI Limitations Logged:** Documenting prompt failure boundaries and ControlNet pose bounds.

### Binding Exit Criteria
> **EXIT CRITERIA:** All 5 animation tests pass the 7-Gate QA Inspection Checklist, demonstrating production-ready visual quality, stable motion, and locked 1.5s reaction hold comedy timing.

---

## 6. Phase 4 — Production Pipeline Validation (10 YouTube Shorts)

Produce and publish **10 Standalone YouTube Shorts** (15–30 seconds each) to validate public distribution, audience retention, and pipeline predictability:

```
  SHORTS 01–03: Core Character Introduction & Cheek-Stuffing Slapstick
  SHORTS 04–07: Water-Bottle Leak Crises & Tube Tube Mishaps
  SHORTS 08–10: 5:00 PM Deadline Pretend-Sleep Transformation
```

### Metrics & Data Tracked
* **Audience Retention Rate (30s):** Target >= 70% retention.
* **Production Speed:** Target 2+ Shorts published per week.
* **Thumbnail CTR:** Target >= 10.5% Click-Through Rate.
* **Audience Feedback:** Analyzing comment sentiment, top rewatched timestamps, and share rates.
* **Iterative Pipeline Tuning:** Apply prompt fixes, sound mixing tweaks, and NLE edit speed improvements after every published Short.

### Binding Exit Criteria
> **EXIT CRITERIA:** Shorts production velocity reaches a predictable 2 Shorts per week at < $15 USD compute cost per Short, with average 30s audience retention exceeding 65%.

## 10. Risk Management & Failure Mitigation

* **Risk 1 (Character Visual Drift During Video Gen):**  
  *Mitigation:* Apply OpenPose ControlNet keyframe guides and locked color mask restoration scripts.
* **Risk 2 (Low Audience Retention on Early Shorts):**  
  *Mitigation:* Shorten Beat 1 (The Hook) to under 3 seconds; increase initial visual punchline speed.
* **Risk 3 (Pipeline Bottleneck During Audio Mix):**  
  *Mitigation:* Pre-record non-verbal soundbanks so audio assembly takes < 15 minutes per Short.
