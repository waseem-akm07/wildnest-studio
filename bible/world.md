# Critter Haven Resort — World Bible

> The single source of truth for the world, rules, locations, and story engine.

---

## The Premise

**Critter Haven Resort** is a secret 5-star luxury pet resort hidden inside a suburban bedroom's plastic hamster cage network.

To the human eye, it's just cages and wood shavings. To the resident hamsters, guinea pigs, geckos, mice, lions, monkeys, owls, pandas, rhinos, and visiting critter guests, it's an exclusive luxury resort where animals live like royalty — but only between **8:00 AM** (when the human leaves) and **5:00 PM** (when the human returns).

### The Core Fantasy

> *"Step inside the secret, cozy magic of a 5-star luxury resort hidden inside a plastic hamster cage, where tiny pets live like royalty the moment their humans leave home."*

---

## 5 Immutable Universe Rules

These rules can **never** be broken in any episode, short, or special:

1. **Secrecy** — Humans must NEVER discover the resort exists. All activity halts instantly when a human enters the room.
2. **The 5:00 PM Ticking Clock** — The resort opens at 8:00 AM (human leaves) and closes at 5:00 PM (human returns). All chaos must be fixed before 5:00 PM.
3. **Scavenged Objects Only** — Every prop must be a creatively re-purposed human object (sock = mattress, thimble = cup, twist-tie = bowtie).
4. **Benign Cartoon Physics** — Characters get squashed and stretched but suffer zero real trauma. They pop right back.
5. **Non-Verbal Communication** — Animals communicate through expressions, gestures, and squeaks — not human speech.

---

## The Story Engine

Every episode is generated through the **Guest & Deadline Engine:**

```
[New Eccentric Guest Checks In] + [Absurd Resort Demand / Failure] + [5:00 PM Ticking Clock] = EPISODE
```

This formula supports infinite episodes. The guest creates the chaos, the staff scrambles to fix it, and the clock creates the tension.

## Daily Life Routine

| Time | Event | What Happens |
|:---|:---|:---|
| **07:30 AM** | Human feeds pets | Staff pretend to be ordinary cage pets eating seeds |
| **08:00 AM** | Human leaves | **RESORT OPENS** — flip welcome sign, start wheels, open tube gates |
| **08:30 AM** | Guest check-in | Scavengers deliver fresh cotton-ball towels; gym opens |
| **12:00 PM** | Lunch rush | Food tube delivery jams; cocktail bar mix-ups |
| **04:30 PM** | Warning chime | Emergency repairs; returning scavenged items to floor |
| **04:55 PM** | Final lockout | Staff jump into sleeping huts; total quiet |
| **05:00 PM** | Human returns | Human: *"Aww, look how peaceful they sleep!"* |

## Economy & Currency

- **Button-Bits** — Shiny plastic buttons serve as currency for room rentals and spa treatments
- **Barter items** — Foil wrappers, rubber bands, popcorn kernels, safety pins
- **Exchange rates:** 1 Lost Gym Sock = 50 Button-Bits (Luxury Mattress), 1 Thimble = 10 Button-Bits (Soup Bowl)

---

## Technology

- **Pneumatic Tube Transit** — Hair-dryers and vacuum exhausts push critter capsules through plastic tubes at high speed
- **Toaster Sauna Heating** — Low-voltage battery wires attached to toaster coils for steam heat
- **Rubber-Band Power Grids** — Stretched rubber-band pulley systems transfer wheel rotation to blenders and elevators

---

## Communication Systems

- **The Pipe Telegraph** — Tapping wooden spoons on plastic tubes transmits morse-like code throughout all wings
- **Paper-Clip Mails** — Rolling tiny paper notes inside plastic beads down gravity tubes
- **Visual Flag Signals** — Miniature cocktail umbrellas on the roof to signal the backyard squad

## What Does NOT Exist

- ❌ Talking humans (humans are only seen as shoes, hands, or distant shadows)
- ❌ Real animal cruelty or trauma
- ❌ Magic or supernatural elements (all tech is mechanical scavenged engineering)
- ❌ Language-dependent humor (must work visually)
- ❌ Photorealistic rendering (strictly stylized 3D)
