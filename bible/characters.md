## Barnaby Q. Whiskers (CHR-001) — Lead Protagonist

**Species:** Golden Syrian Hamster
**Role:** General Manager & Founder of Critter Haven Resort
**The one-liner:** An overly regal, perfectionist hamster who sees himself as a distinguished French hotel concierge — the world sees a frantic chubby hamster in a twisted bread-bag tie.

### Personality

| Attribute | Detail |
|:---|:---|
| **Core Desire** | Run the resort so flawlessly it gets a 5-star rating from every guest |
| **Core Fear** | Being discovered by humans or exposed as incompetent |
| **Core Flaw** | Obsessive micro-management and fragile aristocratic ego — refuses to ask for help until everything is exploding |
| **Ego vs Reality** | Sees himself as a 5-star hotel concierge; the world sees a frantic chubby hamster in a twist-tie |

**OCEAN Ratings:** Openness 5 · Conscientiousness 10 · Extraversion 7 · Agreeableness 6 · Neuroticism 9

### Visual Identity

```
GOLDEN AMBER (60%)     CREAM WHITE (30%)     SIGNAL RED (10%)     COCOA EYES (FOCAL)
┌────────────────┐     ┌────────────────┐    ┌────────────────┐   ┌────────────────┐

└────────────────┘     └────────────────┘    └────────────────┘   └────────────────┘
```

- **Height:** 5.5 inches tall, 1:1.2 head-to-body ratio
- **Shape Language:** 80% Circle (warm/friendly) + 20% Triangle (panic ear tips)
- **Fur:** Warm golden amber back/crown/cheeks, cream white jaw-to-belly patch
- **Eyes:** Large dark cocoa eyes (`#2B1E1A`) with dual white catch-lights, pink button nose (`#FFB7B2`)
- **Paws:** Soft pink 4-fingered front paws (`#FFCAD4`), short chubby rear feet
- **Tail:** Short, pink, 0.5-inch stub that wiggles during anxiety
- **Signature Accessory:** Bright red bread-bag twist-tie bowtie (`#D62828`) — ALWAYS visible

### Comedy Moves

1. **Cheek-Stuffing Panic** — When overwhelmed, stuffs nearby objects into cheek pouches. Head swells to 3x size while trying to speak normally.
2. **The Bowtie Straighten** — After surviving a disaster, immediately dusts off fur and adjusts his tiny red twist-tie bowtie with dignity.
3. **The 1.5-Second Reaction Hold** — Freezes completely for 36 frames (1.5 seconds) with pin-prick pupils before exploding into motion.

### Movement Styles

- **Default (Regal Strut):** Chest out, fast little paw steps, head held high
- **Stealth (Anxious Tiptoe):** Crouched, wide eyes, tip-paws, ears twitching
- **Crisis (Panic Scamper):** Low-to-ground blur run, ears pinned back, tail wiggling
- **Defeat (Slump Walk):** Shoulders slouched, feet dragging, dragging clipboard on floor

### Idle Behaviors

1. Reaching up to center his red twist-tie bowtie
2. A 3-beat rapid whisker twitch, followed by an ear perk
3. Tapping his cheeks to verify nothing is hidden inside
4. Pulling out a tiny scavenged watch gear to check the 5:00 PM countdown

### Emotional Expression Matrix

| Emotion | Eyes | Ears | Body | Action |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| **Joy / Pride** | Wide, sparkling | Perked upright | Chest puffed, paws behind back | Tail wiggle, proud head tilt |
| **Panic / Fear** | Pin-pricks, wide sclera | Flattened against head | Squashed, trembling | Stuffing cheeks with props |
| **Anger** | V-angled, squinted | Angled backward | Paws on hips, foot tapping | Twitching whiskers, foot stomps |
| **Surprise** | O-shaped pop-out | Popped straight up | Jumps 2 inches off ground | Freezes mid-air for 3 frames |
| **Sadness** | Drooped, glassy | Drooped past cheeks | Slouched forward | Fidgeting with twist-tie |
| **Mischief** | Narrowed, smirk | One perked, one tilted | Hunching, rubbing paws | Tiptoeing rhythmically |

### Voice

- **Tone:** Fast-paced, high-pitched, overly formal (like a pompous British butler at 1.5x speed)
- **Non-verbal sounds:** Panic squeak (*"Eeeek!"*), Regal throat clear (*"A-hem!"*), Relief sigh (*"Phew-www..."*), Frustrated mutter (*"Bibble-babble-bumble!"*), Surprised gasp

**Role:** Chief Maintenance Engineer & Lead Mechanic
**The one-liner:** A hyperactive speed-demon who "fixes" problems in 3 seconds flat — but his reckless solutions always trigger the next disaster.

| Attribute | Detail |
|:---|:---|
| **Colors** | Sand Dune Gold (`#E6C280`), Snow White belly (`#FFFFFF`), Charcoal nose (`#333333`) |
| **Height** | 1.8 inches tall, hyper-compact bullet-shaped body |
| **Core Desire** | Fix any mechanical problem instantly using scavenged junk |
| **Core Fear** | Being forced to sit still in a staff meeting |
| **Core Flaw** | Over-engineers simple fixes until they become high-speed hazards |
| **Ego vs Reality** | Thinks he's a Formula-1 pit crew chief; Barnaby sees a twitchy blur causing structural damage |
| **OCEAN** | Extraversion 90 · Neuroticism 15 · Openness 95 · Agreeableness 35 |
| **Accessory** | Scavenged metal paperclip wrapped around forehead like a mechanic's visor |
| **Voice** | High-pitched, raspy, rapid-fire chatter in 3-word Hinglish bursts (*"Boss! Fix ho gaya!"*) |

**Friction Dynamic with Barnaby:**
