## 1. Overview

This document specifies the research standards, evidence evaluation rules, and whitepaper formatting guidelines for all analytical research conducted at **WildNest Studio** inside `research/` and `docs/01_Market_Research/` (derived from `WRS-001`).

---

## 2. Research Principles

1. **First-Principles Reasoning:** Base all creative, business, and pipeline decisions on empirical data, peer-reviewed psychology, industry whitepapers, and verified benchmarks—never unexamined assumptions.
2. **Empirical Evidence First:** Every market or psychological claim must cite authoritative sources (e.g., Aronson et al. for Pratfall Effect, Lorenz for Kindchenschema, YouTube Creator Insights for retention metrics).
3. **Actionable Synthesis:** Research is not academic exercise—every research paper must conclude with concrete, actionable recommendations for WildNest IP, characters, or pipeline workflows.

---

## 3. Whitepaper Document Structure

All studio research whitepapers inside `docs/01_Market_Research/` must adhere to the 6-part standardized layout:

```
1. Executive Summary & Core Hypothesis
2. Empirical Evidence & Academic/Industry Foundations
3. Psychological or Market Mechanics
4. Application to WildNest Flagship IP (Critter Haven Resort)
5. Risk & Devil's Advocate Analysis
6. Actionable Implementation Recommendations & Key Takeaways
```

---

## 4. Source Credibility Hierarchy

| Rank | Source Type | Weight / Credibility |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Tier 1** | Peer-reviewed academic journals (Psychology, Media Studies, AI) | Gold Standard (100%) |
| **Tier 1** | Official platform analytics & industry reports (YouTube, SAG-AFTRA, Variety) | Gold Standard (100%) |
| **Tier 2** | Case studies of successful animation franchises (Pixar, Illumination, Ghibli) | High Credibility (85%) |
| **Tier 3** | Expert interviews, industry podcasts, technical AI benchmarks | Supporting Context (70%) |
| **Forbidden** | Unverified blog posts, speculative social media rumor, un-cited AI summaries | Invalid (0%) |
