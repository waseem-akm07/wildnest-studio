## 1. Overview

This document specifies the core visual design principles, character proportion rules, color management standards, and tactile shader requirements for all **WildNest Studio** visual assets (from `ART-001` and `CHR-BIBLE-001`).

---

## 2. Core Aesthetic Identity

WildNest Studio produces **Stylized Cozy 3D Animation** combining Pixar's character appeal with Studio Ghibli's environmental warmth.

```
[PIXAR 3D CHARACTER APPEAL]  +  [GHIBLI ENVIRONMENTAL WARMTH]  =  WILDNEST COZY 3D TACTILE IDENTITY
```

---

## 3. Character Design & Geometry Rules

### 3.1 Kindchenschema (Baby Schema) Standards
- **Head-to-Body Ratio:** Exaggerated `1:1` to `1:1.2` ratio for mascot characters.
- **Eye Proportion:** Eyes occupy `40%` of vertical head space. Glossy pitch-black pupils with 2 specular highlights.
- **Facial Features:** Tiny soft pink nose (`#FFB7B2`), chubby cheeks designed for cheek-stuffing expansion (up to 3x volume).
- **Body Shape:** Soft pear-shaped silhouette; short stubby limbs.

### 3.2 3-Color Palette Rule per Character
Every character design is strictly anchored by 3 primary color roles:
1. **60% Primary Base:** Primary fur/skin color (e.g., Barnaby Golden Amber `#D48C46`).
2. **30% Secondary Accent:** Chest/belly or secondary fur patch (e.g., Cream White `#FFF8E7`).
3. **10% Focal Pop:** High-contrast accessory or eye color (e.g., Signal Red Twist-Tie `#D62828`).

---

## 4. Scavenged Craft Aesthetic

All props, furniture, and resort structures in *Critter Haven Resort* must follow the **Scavenged Craft Aesthetic**:
- Every prop must be a recognizable, re-purposed human object (e.g., sock mattresses, thimble soup cups, button plates, twist-tie bowties).
- Fantasy or custom manufactured animal-sized props are **forbidden**.
- Materials must show physical wear: crinkles, metallic wire cores, fabric weave, plastic molding seams.

---

## 5. Lighting & Shader Requirements

- **Lighting Pipeline:** 3-Point Warm Cinematic Lighting:
  - **Key Light:** `5500K` Golden Warm Sunlight (50% intensity)
  - **Fill Light:** `6500K` Soft Sky Cyan (30% intensity)
  - **Rim Light:** `3000K` Amber Backlight (20% intensity)
- **Subsurface Scattering (SSS):** Subtle SSS enabled on ears, noses, and paws to convey warm living flesh.
- **Fur Grooming:** Soft peach-fuzz grooming with tactile micro-depth; zero clumpy or photorealistic wire fur.
