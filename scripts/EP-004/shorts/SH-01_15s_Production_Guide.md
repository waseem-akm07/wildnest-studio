# SH-01 Step-by-Step Production Guide (15s)

> **Project:** Critter Haven Resort — SH-01 "Sneaky Snacking Panda"
> **Source Script:** [SH-01_15s_Sneaky_Snacking.md](SH-01_15s_Sneaky_Snacking.md)
> **Format:** YouTube Short (9:16 Vertical)
> **Duration:** 15 seconds (3 Vertical Shots)
> **Target Output:** `output/shorts/EP004_SH01_Sneaky_Snacking.mp4`

---

## 🛠️ RECOMMENDED WORKFLOW

1. **Text-to-Image:** Leonardo.ai / Tensor.art (Canvas: **9:16 Portrait**)
2. **Image-to-Video:** Kling AI / Hailuo AI (Vertical 9:16, 5-second clips)
3. **Voice Lines:** ElevenLabs (Hinglish, Bao & Barnaby)
4. **Editor:** CapCut Mobile or Desktop (9:16 vertical canvas)

---

## STEP 1: GENERATE THE 3 VERTICAL KEYFRAMES

Open **Leonardo.ai**, set aspect ratio to **`9:16` (Vertical)**:

* **Shot 1 (Masterchef Bao):** Copy prompt from `SH-01_15s_Sneaky_Snacking.md` Shot 1 ──► Save as `assets/characters/SH01_EP004_Shot01_Keyframe.png`
* **Shot 2 (Sneaky Bites):** Copy prompt from `SH-01_15s_Sneaky_Snacking.md` Shot 2 ──► Save as `assets/characters/SH01_EP004_Shot02_Keyframe.png`
* **Shot 3 (Big Balloon Gulp):** Copy prompt from `SH-01_15s_Sneaky_Snacking.md` Shot 3 ──► Save as `assets/characters/SH01_EP004_Shot03_Keyframe.png`

---

## STEP 2: ANIMATE THE 3 VERTICAL VIDEO CLIPS

Open **Kling AI** (Image-to-Video mode):

1. Upload `SH01_EP004_Shot01_Keyframe.png` ──► Paste Shot 1 Video Prompt ──► 5s clip
2. Upload `SH01_EP004_Shot02_Keyframe.png` ──► Paste Shot 2 Video Prompt ──► 5s clip
3. Upload `SH01_EP004_Shot03_Keyframe.png` ──► Paste Shot 3 Video Prompt ──► 5s clip

---

## STEP 3: AUDIO & SFX PREPARATION

* **Dialogue:**
  - `SH01_Bao_01.mp3`: *"Five-star feast... ready hoga!"* (Deep, confident rumble)
  - `SH01_Bao_02.mp3`: *"Bas ek taste test... nom-nom!"* (Sneaky, cute)
  - `SH01_Barnaby_01.mp3`: *"Bao! Sab ready?"* (Brisk, hurried)
  - `SH01_Bao_03.mp3`: *"Haan boss!"* (Muffled, guilty)
* **Key SFX:**
  - Rapid chopping sounds (`SFX_Chop.mp3`)
  - Gulp sound (`SFX_Cartoon_Gulp.mp3`)
  - Balloon inflating pop (`SFX_Balloon_Pop.mp3`)
  - Button snap (`SFX_Button_Snap.mp3`)

---

## STEP 4: CAPCUT ASSEMBLY & VERTICAL EXPORT

1. Set Project canvas to **9:16 (1080 x 1920)**.
2. Place Shot 1 (0:00–0:05), Shot 2 (0:05–0:10), Shot 3 (0:10–0:15).
3. Align the audio lines and SFX on exact cuts.
4. On Shot 3, at 0:14.5, freeze frame for 0.5s right as the button snaps, then hard cut to black!
5. Export as `output/shorts/EP004_SH01_Sneaky_Snacking.mp4` (1080x1920, 60fps or 30fps).
