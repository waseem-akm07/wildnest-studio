### Key Short-Form Editing Notes:
1. **The Group Freeze (0:22–0:24):** Cut audio completely for 1.5 seconds except for the clock ticks. This sudden silence creates immense comedic punch on TikTok and Reels.
2. **Text Overlays:** Add dynamic bouncing captions in bold yellow and white (CapCut Auto-Captions with "Bouncy" animation).
3. **Seamless Loop:** The human's affectionate pat and Barnaby's facepalm cleanly loop back to the opening snack hook!
