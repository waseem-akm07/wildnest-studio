# EP-004 Script — "The Great Bao Blockage"

> **Series:** Critter Haven Resort
> **Episode:** EP-004
> **Duration Target:** 2 minutes 30 seconds (150s)
> **Format:** 16:9 Standard Episode (Extractable for 9:16 Shorts)
> **Language:** Hinglish (Max 8 words/line)
> **See:** [concept.md](concept.md)

---

## CAST & ASSETS

* **Barnaby Q. Whiskers** — Golden Syrian Hamster, General Manager (Chubby, red twist-tie bowtie, anxious perfectionist, problem-solver).
* **Bao** — Giant Panda Cub, Head Chef & Comfort Specialist (Massive, gentle, checkered neckerchief, boundless appetite, lovable foodie).
* **Pip** — Roborovski Dwarf Hamster, Lead Mechanic (Tiny, hyperactive, paperclip visor, wrench & rubber bands).
* **Rocco** — White Rhinoceros Calf, Luggage Handler (Gentle giant, yellow caution tape on horn, quiet muscle).
* **Leo** — African Lion Cub, VIP Guest (Dramatic king, gold-foil candy crown, postage-stamp napkin, silver fork and knife).
* **Locations:** Central Lobby Dining Area + Bao's Kitchenette (popsicle-stick counters, seed bins, rebuilt smoothie blender) + Main Tube Corridor (clear plastic connector tunnel).

---

## SCENE 1: LUNCH RUSH ANNOUNCEMENT (0:00 – 0:15)
**[Beat 1: THE HOOK]**
**Location:** Central Lobby Tower & Bao's Kitchenette
**Lighting:** 8:00 AM bright golden morning light.

**ACTION:**
* *CLICK!* Human door shuts upstairs.
* Barnaby pops up from behind the reception counter, straightens his red twist-tie bowtie with pride, and flips the hotel chalkboard: *"LUNCH RUSH — 12:00 PM."*
* He checks his clipboard, grinning ambitiously.

**BARNABY (Proud, energetic):**
> *"Aaj resort ka pehla big feast hai!"*

* Cut to **Bao's Kitchenette**:
* Bao the panda cub stands proudly behind a miniature counter made of wooden toy blocks.
* He wears a crisp red-and-white checkered neckerchief.
* On the counter: piles of sunflower seeds, dried blueberries, miniature diced carrots, and the rebuilt EP-003 smoothie blender gleaming in the sun.
* Bao cracks his chubby knuckles with deep culinary focus.

**BAO (Deep, warm, confident rumble):**
> *"Menu... bilkul five-star hoga, Boss!"*

---

## SCENE 2: THE COOKING & SNEAKY SNACKING (0:15 – 0:45)
**[Beat 2: THE BEND]**
**Location:** Bao's Kitchenette
**Lighting:** Warm kitchen glow, soft morning dust motes floating.

**ACTION:**
* Fast-cut cooking rhythm: Bao is a masterchef at work!
* He expertly slices a dried apple slice on a popsicle-stick cutting board: *CHOP-CHOP-CHOP!*
* He buzzes the smoothie blender: *WHIRRRRR!*
* He arranges three miniature plates with five-star Michelin precision.
* But then... he glances left. He glances right.
* Bao swiftly pops a dried cranberry into his mouth.
* *NOM-NOM-NOM!* His eyes roll back in pure bliss. He pats his round tummy with a contented thud: *THUMP-THUMP.*
* *SQUISH!* His belly expands slightly. His neckerchief tightens by one notch.
* He arranges a bowl of premium toasted sunflower seeds. Looks around again.
* *GULP!* Down goes half the bowl. *NOM-NOM-NOM!*
* *SQUISH!* His tummy bulges another 20%.
* Barnaby bustles past the kitchen doorway holding a guest checklist.

**BARNABY (Fast, brisk manager check-in):**
> *"Bao! Starters ready hai na?"*

* Bao rapidly jams an entire melon chunk behind his back, coughing innocently with puffed cheeks.

**BAO (Muffled, innocent eyes blinking):**
> *"Haan boss! Sab on track hai!"*

* Barnaby nods and hurries away.
* Bao swallows the melon in one giant cartoon gulp: *GULP!*
* *POOF!* Bao's belly balloons outward like an over-inflated basketball. His checkered neckerchief snaps open at the top button.

---

## SCENE 3: THE GREAT TUBE BLOCKAGE (0:45 – 1:15)
**[Beat 3: THE TWIST]**
**Location:** Main Tube Corridor & Dining Area
**Lighting:** Bright daylight through clear curved tube plastic.

**ACTION:**
* Bao waddles proudly down the hallway holding three stacked silver-foil trays above his head.
* He approaches the entrance to the Main Tube Corridor — the primary transit tube connecting the kitchen to the lobby dining room.
* He squeezes into the tube.
* *SQUEEEEAK... SKRRRRRT...*
* He takes one step. Two steps.
* *THWUMP!*
* Bao stops dead. His enormous furry panda belly expands against the curved plastic walls, pressing flush on all 360 degrees like an airtight gasket.
* Bao tries to step forward. His paws paddle the air.
* He tries to step backward. He can't budge.
* He is completely, perfectly, hydraulically wedged like a giant fuzzy cork inside a glass bottle.

**BAO (Small nervous chuckle, straining):**
> *"Uh oh. Tube thoda chhota ho gaya?"*

* **REVEAL THE OTHER SIDE OF THE TUBE:**
* In the Central Lobby Dining Area, **Leo** the lion cub sits at a miniature banquet table.
* Leo wears his shiny gold-foil candy crown and has a postage-stamp napkin neatly tucked into his chest fur.
* In his paws, Leo holds a tiny silver fork and knife upright on the table.
* Leo looks at the grandfather clock on the wall: *12:15 PM.*
* Leo taps his fork impatiently on the porcelain dish: *TINK. TINK. TINK.*
* His tail twitches with royal fury.

**LEO (Dramatic roar-whine, raising fork):**
> *"Mera royal lunch kahan hai?!"*

---

## SCENE 4: CHAOS, THE GROUP FREEZE & CHEEK CLIMB (1:15 – 2:05)
**[Beat 4: THE CHAOS]**
**Location:** Main Tube Corridor (Both Sides)
**Lighting:** Midday emergency sunlight, dramatic comic tension.

**ACTION:**
* **Behind Bao:** Pip arrives on his skateboard, skidding to a halt.
* Pip plants his tiny paws against Bao's enormous furry rear end and shoves with all his hamster might: *PUUUUSH!*
* Pip's feet slide backward along the plastic: *SKRRRR!* Bao doesn't move one millimeter.
* **In Front of Bao:** Rocco the baby rhino arrives, yellow tape glowing on his horn.
* Rocco gently bites Bao's outstretched front paw and leans back with heavy rhino weight: *HEEEAVE!*
* Bao stretches like rubber: *BOOOOING!* But his belly remains cemented to the tube walls.
* Barnaby rushes in, drops his clipboard, and looks at the stuck panda.
* **[1.5-SECOND GROUP REACTION HOLD — SERIES FIRST]**
* Extreme comic deadpan freeze:
* **Barnaby, Pip, and Rocco ALL freeze simultaneously in a row.**
* Three sets of tiny pin-prick pupils stare directly at Bao's squished, wobbling tummy in absolute silence.
* The only sound is the grandfather clock ticking: *TICK... TOCK... TICK.* (Exactly 1.5 seconds).
* Freeze breaks! Barnaby snaps into crisis mode.
* He looks through the clear tube — Leo is tapping his fork aggressively: *TINK-TINK-TINK!*

**BARNABY (Panicked, pacing):**
> *"Leo wait nahi karega! Idea chahiye!"*

* Barnaby spots the dropped food tray on the floor.
* **CHEEK-STUFFING CALLBACK (Stage 2 Evolution):**
* Barnaby grabs dried berries, melon bits, and sunflower seeds.
* He shoves them into his cheek pouches: *STUFF! STUFF! STUFF!*
* His cheeks swell to three times their normal size. His head becomes a giant wobbly triangle.
* With puffed cheeks, Barnaby scales the side of the tube and begins climbing OVER Bao's squishy belly mountain inside the narrow gap!
* Bao's fur tickles: *Hee-hee!*
* Mid-climb, hanging by one paw above Bao's tummy, Barnaby uses his free paw to straighten his red twist-tie bowtie with dignified perfection!
* Barnaby squeezes through the ceiling gap, drops onto the dining table, and unloads a tiny perfect plate right in front of Leo: *PLOP!*

**BARNABY (Muffled cheek voice, polite bow):**
> *"Aapka lunch, Your Majesty!"*

* Leo looks at the single miniature strawberry slice on the plate. Then looks at Barnaby.
* Leo raises an unimpressed royal eyebrow.

**LEO (Pouty royal glare):**
> *"Yeh? Sirf ek bite?!"*

---

## SCENE 5: THE BELLY LAUGH POP & SINCERITY (2:05 – 2:20)
**[Beat 5: THE RESET]**
**Location:** Main Tube Corridor & Kitchen Entrance
**Lighting:** Warm 4:30 PM golden hour light.

**ACTION:**
* Back in the tube, Pip has a stroke of engineering genius.
* He unclips a giant scavenged rubber band from his tool belt and loops it between two metal tube bolts.
* Pip takes a fluffy white cotton ball from his pocket and loads it into the rubber-band slingshot.
* *TWAAAANG!*
* The cotton ball launches down the tube and hits Bao dead-center in his most ticklish belly spot!
* Bao's eyes widen. His ears wiggle.
* A deep, rumbling, tectonic giggle builds in Bao's chest:
* *"Hehehe... HAHAHAHA! HO HO HO!"*
* Bao erupts into an uncontrollable belly laugh!
* His massive stomach vibrates at high frequency: *WOBBLE-WOBBLE-SHAKE!*
* The air seal breaks with a glorious cartoon sound: *SSSSS-THWIP!*
* **POP!** Like a champagne cork shooting out of a bottle, Bao shoots out of the tube!
* *SKIIIIID!* Bao slides across the polished wooden floor and lands in a heap against a pillow pile.
* **THE SINCERITY BEAT:**
* The resort falls completely quiet.
* Bao looks around. The silver food trays are flipped. The soup is spilled. The lunch rush feast is totally ruined.
* Bao's joyful warmth drains completely away. His big furry ears droop down flat against his head.
* He sits alone on the floor, looking at his paws, heartbroken that he ruined Barnaby's big day.

**BAO (Quiet, trembling, heartbroken whimper):**
> *"Sorry, Barnaby... maine sab kharab kiya."*

* Barnaby walks over slowly, cheeks back to normal.
* Bao reaches deep into his checkered neckerchief.
* He pulls out the one treasure he kept safe: a single, golden, perfectly baked sunflower seed cookie shaped like a resort paw-print.
* Bao gently extends both paws, offering the cookie to Barnaby with downcast, guilty eyes.
* Barnaby looks at the cookie. Then looks at Bao.
* Barnaby takes the cookie, snaps off a bite, and chews.
* Barnaby's eyes instantly go wide with pure wonder and warmth.
* Barnaby smiles softly and rests a reassuring paw on Bao's giant arm.

**BARNABY (Soft, heartfelt, warm smile):**
> *"Best cookie ever, Bao. Sab theek hai."*

* Bao's ears perk straight back up. A huge, warm, gummy panda smile spreads across his face.

---

## SCENE 6: THE BEDTIME SQUEEZE (2:20 – 2:30)
**[Beat 6: THE LOOP]**
**Location:** Staff Sleeping Quarters
**Lighting:** 4:59 PM sunset glow turning into cozy evening lamplight.

**ACTION:**
* *JINGLE-CLINK!*
* Heavy human footsteps approach outside. The front door unlocks!
* Barnaby whistles: *TWEET!*
* *"Freeze positions!"*
* Pip dives under an upturned teacup. Rocco tucks into his felt rug corner.
* Bao waddles frantically toward his wooden sleeping crate.
* He dives head-first into the round doorway!
* *THWUMP!*
* His head and shoulders go in... but his round, cookie-stuffed belly gets wedged TIGHT in the crate entrance!
* His big, fluffy black-and-white rear end and tiny round tail stick straight out into the middle of the room.
* Human bedroom door swings open.
* Giant human legs walk into the frame, stopping right by Bao's crate.
* Human bends down, chuckling warmly:

**HUMAN (O.S. Gentle fond voice):**
> *"Aww... Bao, did you eat too many treats again? You chubby boy!"*

* Human gives Bao's stuck rear end a gentle, affectionate double-pat: *PAT-PAT.*
* Bao's tiny tail wiggles back and forth with ecstatic delight.
* In the corner, Barnaby peeks out from a teacup, face-palms with one paw, and straightens his red twist-tie bowtie with the other.
* *FADE TO BLACK.*
