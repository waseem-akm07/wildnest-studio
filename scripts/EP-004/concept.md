## The Idea

**Logline:**
> Bao the panda chef prepares a 5-star feast for the resort's first lunch rush — but eats half the ingredients while cooking, gets impossibly full, and his massive belly blocks the main tube corridor — trapping a furious Leo on the wrong side before 5:00 PM.

## Key Comedy Beats

> Remember: comedy must work on MUTE. Physical > Dialogue. Use BVT (Benign Violation Theory).

1. **The Sneaky Snacking Montage** — Bao cooks with artistic precision, then sneaks bites with guilty side-glances. His belly grows visibly with each snack. By bite #5 his neckerchief barely wraps around him. *"Nom-nom-nom!"* each time.
2. **The Tube Cork** — Bao wedges into the tube perfectly. He rocks back and forth like a giant fuzzy cork. Split-screen comedy: panicked staff on one side, Leo with full table setting on the other, tapping his fork impatiently.
3. **The GROUP 1.5s Freeze** — First time ever: Barnaby, Pip, AND Rocco freeze simultaneously. Three characters, three sets of pin-prick pupils, staring at Bao's belly in a row. Maximum comedy from the escalation.
4. **Cheek-Stuffing Belly Climb** — Barnaby stuffs his cheeks 3x (CALLBACK!), then literally climbs over Bao's stuck belly like a mountain — cheeks wobbling — to deliver a tiny plate to Leo on the other side. New context for the oldest gag = Stage 2 evolution.

---

## Sincerity Beat

After Bao pops free, the feast is ruined — plates shattered, food scattered everywhere. Bao's warm energy completely disappears. He sits amid the wreckage, looking down. For the first time, the happiest character in the cast is genuinely sad. Then he reaches into his checkered neckerchief and pulls out one thing he protected: a single, perfectly shaped sunflower seed cookie — the one dish he didn't eat. He offers it to Barnaby with both massive paws, looking down shyly. Barnaby takes a small bite. His eyes go wide. It's the best thing he's ever tasted. He gives Bao a warm, genuine smile. Bao beams — his warmth floods back. No words needed.
