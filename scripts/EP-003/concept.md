## The Idea

**Logline:**
> Milo the squirrel monkey checks in for a gym session at the Running-Wheel Fitness Center — but his uncontrollable urge to press every button triggers a chain reaction that overloads the resort's entire rubber-band power grid before 5:00 PM.

**Theme:** Curiosity Without Consequences vs. Structure Under Pressure — An unstoppable force of exploration meets an immovable object of scheduled order. The lesson: redirect energy, don't try to cage it.

**Guest Character:** Milo (CHR-004) — Squirrel Monkey — the ultimate chaos catalyst with zero impulse control

## Key Comedy Beats

> Remember: comedy must work on MUTE. Physical > Dialogue. Use BVT (Benign Violation Theory).

1. **The 7-Button Arrival Spam** — Milo presses the tube hatch button 7 times in rapid succession. Each press makes a progressively louder/weirder cartoon sound. Barnaby flinches harder at each one, his bowtie vibrating by the 7th press.
2. **The Rubber Band Tarzan Swing** — Milo grabs a snapped rubber band mid-air with his tail, swings across the gym ceiling like Tarzan, pressing buttons with his free hand mid-flight while Pip chases below on his skateboard, desperately trying to catch up.
3. **Pip's First-Ever 1.5s Freeze** — The Rule of Three subversion. After 2 episodes of Barnaby doing the freeze, EP-003 gives it to the one character who NEVER stops moving. Pip — frozen perfectly still — staring at camera with pin-prick pupils while Milo swings directly behind him. Maximum comedy from the most unexpected character.

---

## Sincerity Beat

After Barnaby lures Milo away from the machines with the shiny rubber band, Milo's hyperactive energy completely drains. He stops. Looks at the sparking, broken equipment around him. Looks up at Barnaby with genuinely guilty, sad monkey eyes — the first time he's been still in the entire episode. No words. 10 full seconds of quiet. Barnaby sighs... then kneels down, takes the rubber band, and gently ties it back around Milo's wrist. A small, forgiving smile. Milo brightens instantly. The bond is formed — not by stopping Milo's curiosity, but by redirecting it.
