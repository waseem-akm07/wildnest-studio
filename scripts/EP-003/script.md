# EP-003 Script — "The Button Maniac"

> **Series:** Critter Haven Resort
> **Episode:** EP-003
> **Duration Target:** 2 minutes 30 seconds (150s)
> **Format:** 16:9 Standard Episode (Extractable for 9:16 Shorts)
> **Language:** Hinglish (Max 8 words/line)
> **See:** [concept.md](concept.md)

---

## CAST & ASSETS

* **Barnaby Q. Whiskers** — Golden Syrian Hamster, General Manager (Chubby, red twist-tie bowtie, anxious perfectionist).
* **Milo** — Squirrel Monkey, Guest / Gym Inspector (Compact, prehensile tail, yellow rubber band on wrist, zero impulse control).
* **Pip** — Roborovski Dwarf Hamster, Lead Mechanic (Tiny, hyperactive, paperclip visor, wrench tool).
* **Bao** — Giant Panda Cub, Head Chef (Massive, gentle, checkered neckerchief — cameo only).
* **Locations:** Central Lobby Tower (reception, pneumatic tube) + Running-Wheel Fitness Center (3 hamster wheels, rubber-band pulleys, smoothie blender, button control panel).

---

## SCENE 1: THE EASY GUEST (0:00 – 0:15)
**[Beat 1: THE HOOK]**
**Location:** Central Lobby Tower Desk
**Lighting:** 8:00 AM bright morning sunbeams.

**ACTION:**
* *CLICK!* Human door shuts.
* Barnaby pops out, straightens his bowtie, flips the sign: *"RESORT OPEN."*
* He picks up his postage-stamp clipboard. Reads today's reservation.
* He shrugs, relaxed for once.

**BARNABY (Confident, casual):**
> *"Milo — Gym pass. Easy hai, aaj."*

* A distant rumbling *WHOOOMP* inside the arrival tube.
* The tube hatch pops open. **Milo** shoots out — lands on all fours.
* Before Barnaby can speak, Milo spots the hatch button.
* He presses it. *BEEP!*
* He presses it again. *BOOP!*
* Again. *BONK!* Again. *DING!* Again. *HONK!* Again. *SQUEAK!* Again. *FOGHORN!*
* **Seven presses in 3 seconds.** Each one louder and weirder than the last.
* Barnaby flinches at every single press, his bowtie vibrating by press #7.

**MILO (Chirpy, fast, delighted):**
> *"Ooh! Button! Ek aur! Ek aur!"*

---

## SCENE 2: THE FITNESS CENTER TOUR (0:15 – 0:45)
**[Beat 2: THE BEND]**
**Location:** Running-Wheel Fitness Center
**Lighting:** Crisp morning light through plastic tube walls, amber rubber-band glow.

**ACTION:**
* Barnaby leads Milo down a tube corridor into the Fitness Center.
* The room is magnificent: 3 giant plastic hamster wheels mounted on wooden axles, connected by colorful rubber-band belts to a scavenged fruit blender (Bao's smoothie station), overhead pulley systems, and a wall-mounted button control panel with red, green, and yellow buttons.
* Milo's eyes go HUGE. His pupils expand to full saucers.
* His tail coils and uncoils with excitement.
* Barnaby steps in front of the control panel, blocking it with his body.

**BARNABY (Very firm, pointing at the red buttons):**
> *"Red buttons mat dabao. Bilkul mat."*

* Milo nods rapidly, standing at attention.
* Behind his back, his prehensile tail silently coils around a red lever on the wall.

**MILO (Innocent face, nodding too fast):**
> *"Haan haan! No red! Promise!"*

* Barnaby gives a suspicious squint. Adjusts his bowtie.
* He turns away for ONE second.

---

## SCENE 3: THE CHAIN REACTION BEGINS (0:45 – 1:15)
**[Beat 3: THE TWIST]**
**Location:** Running-Wheel Fitness Center
**Lighting:** Morning light shifting to warm orange as machines heat up.

**ACTION:**
* Milo's tail PULLS the red lever.
* *CLANK!* Hamster Wheel #1 lurches to life — spinning at **10x normal speed**.
* The rubber-band belt connecting it to Bao's smoothie blender starts smoking.
* *SNAP!* The belt breaks and whips across the room like a slingshot.
* Barnaby ducks just in time — the band grazes his bowtie.
* The disconnected blender keeps spinning from built-up momentum.
* It spews fruit pulp in a wild arc across the gym walls.
* **Bao** appears in the doorway, checkered neckerchief splattered with mango pulp. His mouth hangs open.

**BAO (Devastated, deep gentle voice):**
> *"Mera blender! Smoothie barbaad ho gaya!"*

* Milo, completely unbothered, grins and presses the next button.
* *WHIRR!* Wheel #2 activates.
* His tail finds another lever. *CLANK!* Wheel #3.
* All three wheels are now spinning simultaneously.
* The rubber-band pulley network starts vibrating dangerously.

**BARNABY (Full panic, sprinting toward the panel):**
> *"Ruko! Sab kuch band karo! PIP!"*

---

## SCENE 4: THE TARZAN SWING & PIP'S FREEZE (1:15 – 2:05)
**[Beat 4: THE CHAOS]**
**Location:** Running-Wheel Fitness Center — Full Chaos
**Lighting:** Emergency orange pulsing glow, sparks from overheated rubber bands.

**ACTION:**
* All 3 wheels are at maximum RPM. The rubber-band power grid is overloading.
* Sparks fly from the pulley junction box. Cotton-pad floor mats are sliding around.
* *SCREEEECH!* **Pip** arrives on his toy skateboard, paperclip visor down.
* He immediately starts snipping overheated rubber bands with a paperclip cutter.
* *SNAP!* He cuts Belt #1. The wheel slows.
* But Milo — grinning — reaches over and presses the green restart button.
* *WHIRR!* Wheel #1 is back at full speed.
* Pip cuts Belt #2. Milo presses another button. Belt #2 re-engages.
* Milo is pressing buttons **faster than Pip can cut.**

**PIP (Frustrated, rapid-fire):**
> *"Ye banda rukta hi nahi! Boss, help!"*

* Milo spots a long snapped rubber band dangling from the ceiling pulley.
* His eyes light up. He LEAPS.
* He grabs the rubber band with his tail and swings across the entire gym ceiling — **Tarzan-style!**
* Mid-swing, he uses his free hands to press ceiling-mounted junction buttons — *BEEP-BOOP-DING!*
* Pip stands below, tracking Milo's arc with his head, left... right... left...

**[1.5-SECOND REACTION HOLD — PIP]:**
* Pip freezes. Completely, utterly still.
* For the first time in the series, the hyperactive blur stops moving entirely.
* He stares directly at the camera with pin-prick pupils.
* Complete silence except for Milo's distant *"Ooh-ooh-ah-ah!"* echoing above.
* His paperclip visor slowly slides down over one eye.

---

## SCENE 5: THE RUBBER BAND LURE (2:05 – 2:20)
**[Beat 5: THE RESET]**
**Location:** Running-Wheel Fitness Center
**Lighting:** 4:30 PM warm golden-hour glow.

**ACTION:**
* Barnaby watches Milo swing overhead. He looks at the sparking machines. Then at Pip, still frozen.
* Barnaby's eyes land on something: a shiny yellow rubber band on the floor — the same type as Milo's wrist accessory.
* *LIGHTBULB MOMENT.* Barnaby picks it up.
* He holds the yellow rubber band up and dangles it in the light, making it gleam.
* Milo — mid-swing — spots it. His eyes lock on. Pupils fully dilated.
* He stops pressing buttons. He drops from the ceiling and lands in front of Barnaby.
* He reaches for the rubber band. Barnaby takes one step backward. Milo follows.
* Barnaby leads him, Pied-Piper style, step by step, away from the machines.
* Behind them, Pip snaps out of his freeze, shakes his head, and immediately sprints to the control panel.
* Pip pulls the master emergency lever. All wheels grind to a halt. Silence.

**PIP (Wiping his forehead, exhausted):**
> *"Sab band. Finally."*

* *CLOCK CHIME: 4:45 PM!*
* **[SINCERITY BEAT]:** 10 full seconds.
* Milo stops reaching for the rubber band. He looks around at the sparking equipment, the broken belts, the fruit pulp on the walls.
* His hyperactive energy completely drains. For the first time, he's genuinely still.
* He looks up at Barnaby with big, sad, guilty monkey eyes.
* Silence. No sound. Just Milo looking small.
* Barnaby sighs. A long, tired sigh.
* He kneels down. Takes the rubber band. Gently ties it around Milo's wrist.
* A small, forgiving smile. Milo's eyes brighten. He gives a tiny grateful nod.

---

## SCENE 6: THE TV REMOTE (2:20 – 2:30)
**[Beat 6: THE LOOP]**
**Location:** Bedroom — near the cage
**Lighting:** Cozy warm evening glow.

**ACTION:**
* *CLICK!* The human's front door unlocks.
* Barnaby dives into his sleeping hut. Pip hides behind the wheel.
* Milo sits calmly on a cotton pad. For once, perfectly still.
* Barnaby peeks one eye out and smiles — the guest is finally behaving.
* Then Milo's eyes slowly drift to the right.
* Across the room, on the human's bedside table: the **TV remote**.
* Rows of shiny, colorful, glowing buttons.
* Milo's pupils dilate to full saucers.
* His fingers begin to twitch.

**BARNABY (One eye from the hut, horrified whisper):**
> *"Milo... nahin..."*

* Milo's mouth curls into a slow, mischievous grin.
* Cut to black.
* One single *CLICK!* in the darkness.
* Silence.
* Blackout.
