## Key Comedy Beats

> Remember: comedy must work on MUTE. Physical > Dialogue. Use BVT (Benign Violation Theory).

1. **The Collapsing Shoebox Bed** — Leo lowers himself with maximum royal ceremony, the shoebox flattens like a pancake with a cardboard *CRUNCH*. Leo slowly raises one paw to his forehead and holds it there in absolute theatrical despair.
2. **Rocco's Cotton Ball Blizzard** — Rocco's horn-stack of 50 cotton balls launches into a slow-motion blizzard. All three characters disappear under the fluff. Only Leo's gold-foil crown pokes out of the white pile.
3. **The 1.5-Second Freeze + Crown Landing** — Barnaby coated in fluff attempts regal dignity while staring at the camera. A single cotton ball floats down in slow motion and lands perfectly on Leo's crown. Complete silence. Then Leo gives it a satisfied royal pat.

---

## Sincerity Beat

After the fluffy mountain bed is built and Leo sinks in for the first time, his dramatic persona completely disappears for 10 quiet seconds. He opens one eye, looks at Barnaby — genuinely — and gives a small, wordless nod of appreciation. No joke, no dialogue. Barnaby gives a tiny proud smile back. Then Leo immediately snaps back into character: *"Pillow — fluff it."*
