# EP-002 Script — "The King's Arrival"

> **Series:** Critter Haven Resort
> **Episode:** EP-002
> **Duration Target:** 2 minutes 30 seconds (150s)
> **Format:** 16:9 Standard Episode (Extractable for 9:16 Shorts)
> **Language:** Hinglish (Max 8 words/line)
> **See:** [concept.md](concept.md)

---

## CAST & ASSETS

* **Barnaby Q. Whiskers** — Golden Syrian Hamster, General Manager (Chubby, red twist-tie bowtie, anxious perfectionist).
* **Leo** — African Lion Cub, VIP Guest (Fluffy oversized mane, gold-foil candy wrapper crown, theatrical royalty).
* **Pip** — Roborovski Dwarf Hamster, Lead Mechanic (Tiny, hyperactive, paperclip visor, wrench tool).
* **Rocco** — White Rhinoceros Calf, Luggage Handler (Massive, gentle, yellow construction tape on horn).
* **Locations:** Central Lobby Tower (reception desk, pneumatic tube arrival port) + Bedroom Closet VIP Suite (shoebox interior, thimble side-lamp, cotton-ball mattress).

---

## SCENE 1: THE ROYAL RESERVATION (0:00 – 0:15)
**[Beat 1: THE HOOK]**
**Location:** Central Lobby Tower Desk
**Lighting:** 8:00 AM bright morning sunbeams.

**ACTION:**
* Heavy bedroom door slams shut in the distance: *CLICK!*
* Barnaby pops up from wood shavings, straightens his red twist-tie bowtie.
* He struts to the reception desk and flips the sign: *"SLEEPING"* → *"RESORT OPEN."*
* He picks up his postage-stamp clipboard and reads today's reservation.
* He freezes. His left eye twitches. Once. Twice.
* He turns slowly to stare directly at the camera.

**BARNABY (Reading aloud, then alarmed):**
> *"Leo — Royal Suite — 100 feather bed chahiye."*

*(He stuffs the clipboard under his arm and inhales deeply.)*

---

## SCENE 2: LEO ARRIVES (0:15 – 0:45)
**[Beat 2: THE BEND]**
**Location:** Central Lobby — Pneumatic Tube Arrival Port
**Lighting:** Warm morning ambient, slight golden dust in air.

**ACTION:**
* A distant *WHOOOMP* sound grows louder inside the tube.
* *BOOM!* The arrival-tube hatch flies open.
* **Leo** sails out in a shower of gold glitter — mane first — crown perfectly tilted.
* He lands with one paw extended like a statue, surveys the lobby.
* Barnaby rushes forward, bows stiffly.

**BARNABY (Extremely formal, high-pitched):**
> *"Welcome, welcome! Suite ready hai, Your Magnificence!"*

**LEO (Booming theatrical declaration):**
> *"Hmph. Show me my royal chambers."*

* Barnaby leads Leo through a narrow tube to the Closet VIP Suite.
* Leo steps inside the shoebox suite. He scans the room slowly.
* He spots the "bed" — a single cotton ball on cardboard.
* Leo reaches in with two precise claws. He lifts the lone cotton ball.
* He holds it up. He stares at it. He stares at Barnaby.
* One eyebrow rises magnificently.

**LEO (Deadly quiet, horrified):**
> *"Ek... cotton ball?"*

---

## SCENE 3: THE ROYAL COLLAPSE (0:45 – 1:15)
**[Beat 3: THE TWIST]**
**Location:** Bedroom Closet VIP Suite
**Lighting:** Morning light through closet crack — soft shadows.

**ACTION:**
* Leo waves a dismissive royal paw.
* With maximum ceremony, he lowers himself onto the shoebox "bed."
* He descends slowly... slowly... *CRUNCH-CRUNCH-CRAAAACK!*
* The shoebox collapses completely flat like a pancake.
* Leo sits on the floor, cardboard splayed around him.
* He raises one paw to his forehead. He holds it there.
* A single dramatic tear forms in the corner of his eye.

**LEO (Theatrically devastated):**
> *"Main... main toot gaya. Emotionally."*

* Barnaby stares at the flattened shoebox.
* He adjusts his bowtie. Looks at camera. Back at Leo.
* He turns and sprints out at full panic speed.

**BARNABY (Sprinting, muffled panic):**
> *"Pip! Rocco! Emergency hai! Jaldi aao!"*

---

## SCENE 4: THE COTTON BALL BLIZZARD (1:15 – 2:05)
**[Beat 4: THE CHAOS]**
**Location:** Central Lobby / Corridor Tubes
**Lighting:** 1:00 PM midday warm light, emergency orange beginning.

**ACTION:**
* Barnaby, Pip, and Rocco assemble in the lobby.
* Pip points at the giant cotton-ball supply bag in the corner.
* Rocco nods eagerly and carefully stacks 50 cotton balls on his horn — balancing with surprising delicacy.
* He takes one step forward. His foot snags on a loose rubber band on the floor.
* *TRIP!* Rocco lurches. The cotton tower wobbles.

**ROCCO (Gentle gasp, horrified):**
> *"Nahin nahin nahin nahin—"*

* *WHOOOMP!* The entire cotton stack launches skyward.
* **SLOW MOTION:** 50 cotton balls burst into the air like a white blizzard.
* The entire lobby fills with floating white fluff.
* Barnaby, Pip, and Rocco disappear completely under the cotton cloud.
* Silence. A mountain of white fluff.
* Only Leo's gold-foil crown pokes out from the pile.

**[1.5-SECOND REACTION HOLD]:**
* The fluff settles. Barnaby's head emerges from the white pile.
* He is coated head-to-toe in cotton fluff. He looks like a tiny snowman.
* He stares directly at the camera with pin-prick pupils. Complete silence.
* A single remaining cotton ball floats down from the ceiling... slowly... slowly...
* *POOF.* It lands perfectly on top of Leo's tilted crown.
* Leo opens one eye, looks at it, and gives it a single satisfied royal pat.

---

## SCENE 5: THE ROYAL BED IS BORN (2:05 – 2:20)
**[Beat 5: THE RESET]**
**Location:** Bedroom Closet VIP Suite
**Lighting:** 4:30 PM warm golden-hour glow.

**ACTION:**
* *CLOCK CHIME: 4:30 PM!*
* All three staff explode into speed-cleaning mode.
* Pip zips back and forth collecting cotton balls at ultra-speed.
* Rocco carefully, painstakingly places each ball one by one (ballerina-level precision this time).
* Barnaby frantically pats and fluffs the growing mountain into a glorious 5-star bed.
* Together they step back. The bed is **magnificent** — a towering cotton-mountain with a postage-stamp gold nameplate that reads *"ROYAL SUITE."*
* Leo enters the rebuilt suite. His eyes go wide.
* He lowers himself into the cotton mountain. He sinks in with a long, slow, theatrical sigh.

**LEO (Eyes closed, deeply satisfied):**
> *"...Theek hai. Acceptable."*

* **[SINCERITY BEAT]:** 10 full seconds of silence.
* Leo opens one eye. He looks at Barnaby — no drama, no theatre.
* He gives a slow, genuine, tiny nod. Just for Barnaby.
* Barnaby's chest swells with quiet pride. He gives one small nod back.
* Then Leo straightens and snaps back into royal mode.

**LEO (Immediately imperious):**
> *"Ab pillow fluff karo. Dono side se."*

**PIP (Grinning, energetic):**
> *"Five minutes bache hain, boss!"*

---

## SCENE 6: THE ROYAL REFUSES TO HIDE (2:20 – 2:30)
**[Beat 6: THE LOOP]**
**Location:** Bedroom Closet VIP Suite
**Lighting:** Cozy warm evening glow.

**ACTION:**
* *CLICK!* The human's front door unlocks.
* Barnaby's ears snap straight up.
* Pip dives behind a thimble. Rocco tries to crouch — fails — stays frozen.
* Barnaby frantically waves at Leo. *Hide! Hide!*
* Leo opens one regal eye. He does not move.
* Barnaby stares at Leo. Then at the door. Then at the camera.
* **[REACTION HOLD — 1 SECOND]**: Pin-prick pupils. Absolute dread.
* Human footsteps approach. A giant hand reaches in and strokes the fluffy cotton-mountain Leo is sitting on.

**HUMAN VOICE (Off-screen, delighted):**
> *"Aww — look at that cozy little nest!"*

* The human withdraws. Footsteps fade.
* Leo closes both eyes in pure smug satisfaction.
* He straightens his gold-foil crown.
* Barnaby slowly face-palms with both paws.
* He sighs, stands tall, and adjusts his red twist-tie bowtie with dignity.
* He smirks at camera. Blackout.
