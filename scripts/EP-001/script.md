# EP-001 Script — "The Grand Opening Chaos"

> **Series:** Critter Haven Resort  
> **Episode:** EP-001 (Pilot)  
> **Duration Target:** 2 minutes 30 seconds (150s)  
> **Format:** 16:9 Standard Episode (Extractable for 9:16 Shorts)  
> **Language:** Hinglish (Max 8 words/line)  
> **See:** [concept.md](concept.md)

---

## CAST & ASSETS

* **Barnaby Q. Whiskers** — Golden Syrian Hamster, General Manager (Chubby, red twist-tie bowtie, anxious perfectionist).
* **Pip** — Roborovski Dwarf Hamster, Lead Mechanic (Tiny, hyperactive, wrench tool).
* **Location:** Central Lobby Tower (Multi-level plastic cage, tea saucer reception desk, sunflower seed bar, translucent tube network).

---

## SCENE 1: THE RESORT OPENS (0:00 – 0:25)
**[Beat 1: THE HOOK]**  
**Location:** Central Lobby Tower Desk  
**Lighting:** 8:00 AM bright morning sunbeams through human bedroom window.

**ACTION:**
* Heavy bedroom door clicks shut: *SLAM!* (Human departure sound).
* Barnaby pops out from a mountain of soft wood shavings.
* He proudly straightens his red twist-tie bowtie with tiny pink paws.
* Struts up to the upside-down tea saucer reception desk.
* He flips a mini cardboard sign from *"SLEEPING"* to *"RESORT OPEN"*.

**BARNABY (Proud, formal):**
> *"Human gaya! Resort open karo, chalo!"*

---

## SCENE 2: THE ROUTINE INSPECTION (0:25 – 0:50)
**[Beat 2: THE BEND]**  
**Location:** Lobby Tube Line 1  
**Lighting:** Crisp morning ambient light.

**ACTION:**
* Barnaby waddles down Tube Line 1 with a tiny clipboard (a postage stamp).
* He inspects the crystal-clear plastic walls, nodding with pompous satisfaction.
* Suddenly: *DRIP.* A single droplet of water lands squarely on his forehead.
* Barnaby looks up with wide, trembling eyes.
* A rubber gasket in the ceiling tube is bulging slightly.

**BARNABY (Nervous murmur):**
> *"Ek boond? No problem, main sambhal loonga."*

* Barnaby reaches up on his tiptoes and tries to push the rubber gasket back with his tiny thumb.

---

## SCENE 3: THE GASKET EXPLOSION (0:50 – 1:30)
**[Beat 3: THE TWIST]**  
**Location:** Tube Line 1 / Central Atrium  
**Lighting:** Bright reflections on splashing water.

**ACTION:**
* *POP!* The gasket ruptures completely.
* A high-pressure jet of water sprays Barnaby directly in the face!
* Water starts swirling around his paws, quickly rising up to his knees.
* Barnaby frantically grabs 3 metal sewing thimbles and a miniature foam sponge.
* He stuffs everything into his cheek pouches to block the spray.
* His cheeks swell **3x normal size** like giant fuzzy balloons!

**BARNABY (Muffled through stuffed cheeks):**
> *"Mmmph! Pip kahan hai?! Tube phat gaya!"*

---

## SCENE 4: WATER JET HELICOPTER & THE FREEZE (1:30 – 2:05)
**[Beat 4: THE CHAOS]**  
**Location:** Lobby Tube Junction  
**Lighting:** Dynamic water reflections, emergency orange glow starting.

**ACTION:**
* The water pressure builds to critical level: *WHOOOOOSH!*
* Barnaby is launched backward through the spiral tube like a fuzzy torpedo.
* As he flies through mid-air, his red twist-tie bowtie spins at 1,000 RPM like a helicopter rotor!
* *SPLASH!* He lands belly-first in the lobby cotton pile.
* He sits up drenched, hair sticking in funny spikes.
* A giant, wobbly water bubble hovers directly above his head.
* **[1.5-SECOND REACTION HOLD]**: Barnaby stares directly at the camera with pin-prick pupils. Complete silence except for a tiny squeak.
* *POP!* The water bubble bursts on his head, soaking him flat.

---

## SCENE 5: PIP'S RESCUE & SPEED CLEAN (2:05 – 2:20)
**[Beat 5: THE RESET]**  
**Location:** Lobby Desk / Tube Line 1  
**Lighting:** 4:30 PM warm golden hour orange glow.

**ACTION:**
* A tiny blur zooms in with squealing tires — **Pip** on a modified toy skateboard!
* Pip loads a giant plastic shirt button into a rubber-band slingshot.
* *TWANG — THUNK!* The button slams into the pipe leak, sealing it airtight.
* The water stops instantly.
* **[SINCERITY BEAT]**: Barnaby looks at the sealed pipe, then down at Pip. He offers a slow, genuine nod of deep respect.
* *CLOCK CHIMES: 4:55 PM!* (Ticking clock panic).
* Both immediately grab miniature cotton pads and speed-mop the entire floor at 2x fast-forward speed.

**PIP (Grinning, energetic):**
> *"Five minutes bache hain! Jaldi saaf karo!"*

---

## SCENE 6: THE 0.1-SECOND PRETEND SLEEP (2:20 – 2:30)
**[Beat 6: THE LOOP]**  
**Location:** Bedroom / Sleeping Hut  
**Lighting:** Cozy evening room lighting.

**ACTION:**
* *CLICK!* The human's front door unlocks in the distance.
* Barnaby dives headfirst into his wooden sleeping hut in 0.1 seconds.
* Instantly, large cartoon snoring bubbles float out of his nose: *Zzzzzz...*
* The human footsteps approach and pause by the cage.
* **HUMAN VOICE (Off-screen):** *"Aww, look at Barnaby. He's been sleeping all day!"*
* The human walks away.
* Barnaby opens one sly eye, straightens his bowtie, and gives a confident smirk to the camera.
