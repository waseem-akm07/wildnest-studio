# EP-001 — "The Grand Opening Chaos"

---

## Episode Info

- **Episode ID:** EP-001 (Pilot)
- **Title:** The Grand Opening Chaos
- **Format:** Standard Episode
- **Duration:** 2 minutes 30 seconds (150s)

---

## The Idea

**Logline:**
> Barnaby attempts a flawless grand opening of Critter Haven Resort the moment his human leaves — but a sudden water-bottle tube leak threatens to flood the Central Lobby Tower before 5:00 PM.

**Theme:** Obsessive Perfectionism vs. Adaptive Teamwork under Pressure

**Guest Character:** None (pilot focuses on establishing Barnaby and the resort)

---

## The 6-Beat Outline

| Beat | What Happens | Time |
|:---|:---|:---|
| **1. HOOK** | Human door closes at 8:00 AM. Barnaby emerges in his red bowtie, inspects the pristine lobby, proudly flips the "RESORT OPEN" sign. Title card. | 0:00–0:15 |
| **2. BEND** | Barnaby does a routine tube inspection. Discovers a single water droplet dripping from a gasket in Tube Line 1. Tries to fix it himself. | 0:15–0:40 |
| **3. TWIST** | The gasket BURSTS. Water sprays everywhere. Barnaby tries to block the leak with his paws, then stuffs 3 thimbles and a sponge into his cheeks (head swells 3x). The lobby starts flooding. | 0:40–1:15 |
| **4. CHAOS** | A water jet shoots Barnaby backward through a tube (his bowtie spins like a helicopter propeller). He surfaces with pin-prick pupils. 1.5-SECOND REACTION HOLD as a giant water bubble hovers over his head before popping on him. Total waterlogged chaos. | 1:15–2:05 |
| **5. RESET** | Pip the Dwarf Mechanic zooms in, launches a rubber-band button plug at the leak. THUNK — it seals perfectly. 4:30 PM chime sounds. Together they speed-clean the lobby. | 2:05–2:20 |
| **6. LOOP** | At 4:59 PM, Barnaby dives into his sleeping hut. Instantly produces loud snoring bubbles 0.1 seconds before the human door opens. Human: *"Aww, look how peaceful!"* — Barnaby opens one eye, adjusts his bowtie, and smirks. | 2:20–2:30 |

## Characters Used

- **Lead:** Barnaby Q. Whiskers (CHR-001)
- **Supporting:** Pip (CHR-002) — arrives in the Reset beat to save the day
- **Guest:** None (pilot episode)

---

## Key Comedy Beats

1. **Cheek-Stuffing Panic** — Barnaby stuffs 3 thimbles and a sponge into his cheeks to block the water. Head swells to 3x size while he tries to speak normally.
2. **The Helicopter Bowtie** — Water jet launches Barnaby backward through a tube, his red twist-tie spins like a propeller.
3. **The 1.5s Freeze** — Barnaby stares directly at camera with pin-prick pupils as a giant water bubble hovers over his head... then pops.
4. **The Instant Pretend-Sleep** — 4:59 PM chime; Barnaby transforms from frantic cleaning to peaceful snoring in 0.1 seconds flat.

---

## Sincerity Beat

After Pip seals the leak, Barnaby looks at the fixed pipe, then at Pip. He gives a small, genuine nod of gratitude — no joke, no dialogue. Just a quiet moment of respect between manager and mechanic. Then Pip ruins it by accidentally knocking over a thimble tower.

- All 3 core comedy moves (cheek-stuffing, bowtie straighten, 1.5s hold)
