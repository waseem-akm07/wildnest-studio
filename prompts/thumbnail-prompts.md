# Thumbnail Generation Prompts

> Prompts and rules for creating YouTube thumbnails that get clicks.

---

## The 3-Element Rule

Every thumbnail must have these 3 elements:

```
60% — 1-2 LARGE HIGH-EMOTION CHARACTER FACES (Panic, Shock, Joy)
30% — HIGH-CONTRAST BRIGHT BACKGROUND (cyan tube or orange glow)
10% — 1 ICONIC PROP (exploding clock gear, dripping water bottle)
```

**Text Rule:** Zero or max 2 bold words in rounded 3D yellow type (`#FFD166`)

---

## Master Thumbnail Prompt

```
YouTube thumbnail, extreme close-up of [CHARACTER] with [EMOTION] expression
(wide eyes, [specific facial detail]). [BACKGROUND COLOR/SCENE].
[ICONIC PROP] in frame. Bold 3D yellow text "[1-2 WORDS]".
Vibrant saturated colors, bright studio lighting, clean composition,
high contrast, 1920x1080 --ar 16:9
```

### Example: EP-001 Thumbnail

```
YouTube thumbnail, extreme close-up of Barnaby the golden hamster with
wide panicked pin-prick pupils and stuffed puffy cheeks. Bright cyan plastic
tube background with water splashing. A giant dripping water bottle in frame.
Bold 3D yellow text "OH NO!". Vibrant saturated colors, bright studio lighting,
clean composition, high contrast, 1920x1080 --ar 16:9
```

---

## Thumbnail Checklist

- [ ] Character face fills 60% of the frame?
- [ ] Expression is EXTREME (panic, shock, joy — not subtle)?
- [ ] Background is bright and high-contrast?
- [ ] One prop tells the story at a glance?
- [ ] Text is max 2 words and easy to read at small sizes?
- [ ] Readable at mobile phone thumbnail scale?
