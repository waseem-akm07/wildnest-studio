# Voice Generation Prompts

> Prompts for generating character voices using AI text-to-speech tools.

---

## Barnaby Voice

```
High-pitched, fast-cadence, articulate male voice with an overly formal, polite tone
and high emotional reactivity. Expresses cheerful formality when calm, shifting into
rapid, squeaky, breathy panic during high tension. Non-verbal squeaks, throat clears,
and sighs interspersed naturally.
```

**Non-verbal sound library:**
1. Panic squeak: *"Eeeek!"*
2. Regal throat clear: *"A-hem!"*
3. Relief sigh: *"Phew-www..."*
4. Frustrated mutter: *"Bibble-babble-bumble!"*
5. Surprised gasp: Sharp intake with squeaky pop

---

## Pip Voice

```
High-pitched, raspy, rapid-fire chatter voice. Extremely fast delivery.
Speaks in short 3-word Hinglish bursts. Confident and unfazed.
```

---

## General Voice Guidelines

- All dialogue follows **Hinglish** (Hindi grammar + English loanwords)
- Max **8 words per spoken line**
- Each character gets **8 non-verbal sounds**: squeak, gasp, groan, chirp, hum, grunt, sigh, giggle
- Voices should reflect character personality through **delivery and timing**, not complex vocabulary
