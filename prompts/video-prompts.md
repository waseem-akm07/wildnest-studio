Smooth 24fps Pixar-style animation, warm studio lighting,
subtle depth of field, detailed fur movement --v 6.0

## Tips for Video Prompts

1. Always specify **"24fps"** and **"smooth animation"**
2. Describe the **specific action sequence** clearly (start → middle → end)
3. Keep clips SHORT — 3-5 seconds per generation works best with current AI
4. Include the **"1.5-second reaction hold"** for comedy beats
5. Reference the character's **colors and accessories** for consistency
6. Add **"Pixar-style"** to maintain the art direction
